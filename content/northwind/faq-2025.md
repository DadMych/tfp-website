# Frequently asked questions (2025 version, superseded)

**This FAQ is superseded and no longer in force.** It was the client FAQ in 2025. Do not send it. The current FAQ replaces it. The three answers people still quote from this file are wrong. They are kept here so we can recognise an old email when we see one.

## Can we pause?

Then: once per year, up to one month.

That answer is superseded. The current rule is once per year, up to two months, with 14 days' notice before the next invoice.

## Do you discount non-profits?

Then: a registered non-profit received 10% off retainers.

That figure is superseded. The current non-profit discount is 15% off retainers. It still cannot be combined with the annual prepayment discount.

## Do you do naming?

Then: naming was an add-on to a Brand Sprint at €2,000, for up to three name candidates.

That price is superseded. The current naming add-on is €2,500, still Brand Sprint only, still up to three name candidates. The final trademark check stays with the client.

## Answers from 2025 that are easy to mix up

These were true when this page was current. Check the date of the agreement before you repeat them.

- Two revision rounds per deliverable. The older rule of three rounds had already ended on 1 January 2025.
- Unused retainer hours rolled over for one month and were not refunded.
- Payment terms were 14 days. A rush, in 2025, was confirmed in writing by the account lead. The rush fee then was 25%.
- Support: Starter within 2 working days, Studio within 1 working day. There was no Partner plan and no urgent line.
- Retainer prices on the 2025 list were Starter €2,100 and Studio €5,200. This FAQ did not repeat the whole price list.

If a client attaches this file, send them the current FAQ and say this one is superseded.
