Hi Lena,

A downgrade needs 30 days' written notice to me. It does not start on the next invoice the way an upgrade does. If you reply to confirm, that reply is the notice, and Starter applies once the 30 days have run.

What changes when it does:

- Price goes from €5,800 a month to €2,400, excl. VAT.
- Hours go from 64 to 24. Active requests go from 2 to 1. If two requests are still open, we will choose which one stays active.
- First reply on support moves from the same working day (Studio) to within 2 working days (Starter).

Rolled-over hours do not carry across in full on a downgrade. They are capped to 24, which is Starter's monthly allowance. Anything above that cap does not move. On an upgrade it would be the other way round, but that is not this change.

Unused hours are not refunded, on either plan. Your current Studio month runs on Studio terms until the notice ends.

Reply if you want me to start the 30 days.

Best,
Sandra
