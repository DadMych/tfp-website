Hello,

Yes, as an add-on to the Brand Sprint, not as its own project. The naming add-on is €2,500. It covers up to three name candidates. It is only available on a Brand Sprint, which is the engagement you're already in.

What you get from us is the three candidates, a short rationale for each, and basic trademark guidance: the sort of check that tells you whether a name looks obviously crowded, and what you should ask a trademark attorney. The final trademark check is yours. We are not a law firm, and we don't file anything.

The Brand Sprint itself stays what it is: logo, colour, type, a 30-page brand book, and one simple logo animation, over 4 weeks, at €9,500. Naming doesn't replace any of that. If you pick a new name halfway through, the logo work follows the name you choose. If you keep the name you have, the identity work continues against it. Either outcome is a normal result of the add-on.

Say the word and I'll add it to the statement of work. The €2,500 sits on top of the Brand Sprint. It doesn't replace the sprint, and it isn't available on its own.

Sandra Tsapljoek
Account lead, Northwind Studio
