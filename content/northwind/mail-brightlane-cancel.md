Hello,

Cancelling needs 30 days' written notice, by email to me. The retainer runs through that notice. Work in flight finishes or stops where you tell us to stop; it doesn't vanish on the day you write.

Unused hours are not refunded. They roll one month and then expire, whether you stay or leave. That includes any balance left at the end.

You own the final files once they're paid for, which on a retainer they are as each month is paid. Export what you want before the access goes. Figma access is removed 30 days after the end. Those are two different periods: 30 days' notice to end, and then 30 days after the end before the Figma access comes off. Files themselves are kept 24 months after the last invoice, so an export is still possible after the Figma seat is gone, until that retention runs out.

If the retainer had been prepaid for the year, unused months would be refunded minus the discount received for them. I'm not treating this note as notice, and I'm not assuming you prepaid a year. Ask Vladislava if you want the invoice history checked before you decide.

A pause is the other door: once a year, up to two months, with 14 days' notice before the next invoice. Say if that's the actual question.

Vladislav Boerdonos
Account lead, Northwind Studio
