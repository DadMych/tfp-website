# Handbook: onboarding a new client (internal)

*Internal. Last updated 2 October 2025. The account lead owns this. Vladimir Posjivalov handles the contract, and ops helps with the Harbor account. Designers don't start a file "to be ready" before these steps exist.*

Do these in order. Skipping the Notion page is how we lose track of who approves work and whether we can show it later.

## 1. Agreement, then the intake form

Nothing else starts until the agreement is signed. Send the intake form with the countersigned copy, not before. The form is how we learn who signs off, what they think the work is, and whether they want a confidentiality clause. That clause is free at signing. If they want it, it has to be in the agreement, not promised in an email afterwards.

## 2. Harbor account

Create the Harbor account before kickoff. Requests, files, and invoices live there. If the client emails a brief to someone they met on the intro call, the account lead moves it into Harbor. We don't run the project from an inbox.

## 3. Figma project

One Figma project per client. Pages, in this order, even if some are empty:

- Cover
- Explorations
- Final
- Handoff

Leave Explorations in the file. Don't delete it because the first week looks bare. Name the file the way the file-naming page says.

## 4. Notion client page

Create the Notion page the same day as the Figma project. Record the plan or the project, the account lead, the people on their side who approve, and whether there is a confidentiality clause. Portfolio questions get answered from this page later. If the clause isn't written here, it will be forgotten.

## 5. Kickoff

Book the kickoff within 10 working days of signing. The account lead holds the date. If the calendar can't fit it, say so in the first week. Don't let day eleven arrive and call it a scheduling surprise.

The designer who will do the work should be in the kickoff, not briefed second-hand the week after.

## 6. Where the tasks go

Open the work in Linear so requests have a home that isn't a private to-do list. Link the Harbor request, the Figma file, and the Notion page from the ticket.

## Who does what

- Account lead: intake, Harbor, kickoff date, the Notion page, moving stray email into Harbor.
- Vladimir Posjivalov: the contract.
- Ops (Maksim Ryzjikov): first invoice. Retainers are invoiced on the first working day of the month. Fixed-price projects are 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery.
- Design: the Figma project, and showing up to kickoff.

If a step is blocked, write it on the Notion page the same day. "Waiting on their legal" is a status. Silence is not.
