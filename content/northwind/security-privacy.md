# Security and privacy

## Where your files live

Project files are stored in Figma and in our client portal, both hosted in the European Union. We don't keep copies on personal laptops beyond what the design tools cache.

## How long we keep them

We keep project files for 24 months after the last invoice, then delete them. If you want them deleted sooner, ask your account lead in writing; we confirm deletion within 10 working days. Invoices are kept for 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires.

## NDAs

We sign mutual NDAs before any project on request, using our template or yours. Everyone at Northwind is under a confidentiality clause in their employment contract.

## Access after the project

When a project or retainer ends, we remove your team's access to our Figma project after 30 days. Export what you need before then, or ask us for a full export.

## AI tools

We don't upload client files or confidential briefs to public AI tools. Any AI-generated imagery in a deliverable is labelled as such and only used with your approval.
