# Creative brief: Quill Notes brand sprint

Internal brief for the sprint team. Written 26 January 2026, before kickoff. Quill Notes is a note-taking app. The engagement is a Brand Sprint (NW-SPR-B) in 2026, plus the naming add-on.

## Background

The product has a name and a working app. The identity has not kept up with how people use it. Quill wants a brand they can apply in the product, on the site, and in the few places a small team actually publishes. This is a fixed-price sprint, not a retainer. Commercial terms are the standard ones: 50% at kickoff, 50% on delivery, payment within 14 days. The sprint fee and the naming add-on are quoted separately. Do not roll them into one number in the deck.

## Goals

The identity should feel calm and precise. Calm means it does not shout, and it still works at the size of an app icon. Precise means type, colour and logo rules a small team can follow without us in the room.

Naming is an open question at kickoff, not a decision. The add-on explores whether another name would serve the product. The client may keep Quill Notes. We do not steer them toward a change.

## Audience

Students and researchers. They live in the product for long stretches of reading and writing, so the identity has to stay quiet next to their own text. We are not briefing a consumer lifestyle brand.

Tone for the work we show them: calm, precise. Same words we will use in the brand book.

Competitors: none named. Do not add competitor logos to the explorations unless the client names someone in writing after kickoff.

## Deliverables

Per NW-SPR-B:

- Logo
- Colour
- Type
- A 30-page brand book
- One simple logo animation

Naming add-on, Brand Sprint only: up to three name candidates, at €2,500. Basic trademark guidance only. The client does the final trademark check. We are not their lawyers.

Two revision rounds are included on each deliverable. A further round on this fixed-price project is €450. Feedback is due within 5 working days.

## Timeline

Kickoff is 2 February 2026. Delivery is 2 March 2026. That is the four weeks in NW-SPR-B. Both dates are Mondays, inside normal studio hours.

The logo animation is part of delivery, not a follow-on. Book it inside the four weeks.
