# Master Services Agreement (2026 template)

This is the Northwind Studio master services agreement template dated 1 January 2026. It is the starting point for engagements signed or renewed from that date. If a signed agreement differs from this template, the signed agreement prevails. Prices in this template are exclusive of VAT. Northwind Studio is established in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, and this template does not create an office anywhere else.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Agreement" means this master services agreement together with any statement of work or order signed under it.

1.2 "Client" means the company or organisation named in the statement of work.

1.3 "Northwind" means Northwind Studio, the design agency founded in 2017, with its office in Lloydkwartier, Rotterdam.

1.4 "Services" means the design services described in clause 2, as narrowed by the statement of work.

1.5 "Deliverable" means a discrete piece of design work identified in a statement of work or in a request accepted through Harbor.

1.6 "Retainer" means a monthly engagement on the Starter, Studio, or Partner plan, under which the Client receives a budget of design hours and a limit on active requests.

1.7 "Fixed-Price Project" means a Brand Sprint, a Website Sprint, a Design System Audit, or another engagement priced as a single fee rather than by the month.

1.8 "Design Hour" means an hour of design time counted against a Retainer. Hours are a budget, not a promise of a particular output.

1.9 "Round" means one consolidated set of revisions to a Deliverable, as described in clause 6.

1.10 "Account Lead" means the Northwind person named as responsible for the Client's relationship. Notice under this Agreement is given to that person unless a clause says otherwise.

1.11 "Harbor" means Northwind's client portal for requests, files, and invoices.

1.12 "Final Files" means the delivered files of a Deliverable in the formats Northwind hands over at the end of that Deliverable, and not drafts, rejected concepts, or unused explorations.

1.13 "Confidential Information" means information that a reasonable person would treat as private to the disclosing party, including briefs, unreleased product, and the contents of the files, whether or not marked confidential.

## 2. Services

2.1 Northwind provides brand identity, web design, design systems, and product design for software companies.

2.2 Northwind does not write production code, does not build mobile applications, does not run advertising, does not manage social media, and does not produce video other than a simple logo animation where a Brand Sprint includes one. Northwind does not subcontract development. If the Client engages a developer, that contract is between the Client and the developer.

2.3 The tools used to perform the Services include Figma for design and Harbor for requests, files, and invoices. A statement of work may name the people assigned. On the Partner plan the assignment includes a second senior designer and a monthly review call with the studio director.

2.4 Northwind's working hours are 09:00 to 18:00 CET, Monday to Friday. Northwind is closed on Dutch public holidays and from 24 December to 2 January. In 2026 Northwind is closed on 1 and 2 January, Good Friday 3 April, Easter Monday 6 April, King's Day 27 April, Liberation Day 5 May, Ascension Day 14 May, Whit Monday 25 May, and 24 to 31 December. Requests sent on a closed day start on the next working day.

2.5 Northwind plans kickoff within 10 working days of signing.

2.6 Nothing in this Agreement obliges Northwind to produce a stated number of screens, components, or other outputs from a given number of hours. The hours and the fees buy time and attention within the scope. They do not buy a promise that a named feature will be finished.

## 3. Requests, turnaround, and support

3.1 Requests are submitted through Harbor, one request for a Deliverable. Northwind is not obliged to start work that arrives only by email or by a side message.

3.2 On a Retainer, standard turnaround is a start within 3 working days and a first version within 7 working days, unless clause 7 (rush) has been confirmed in writing.

3.3 First replies on support questions follow the plan. Starter: within 2 working days. Studio: the same working day. Partner: within 4 working hours.

3.4 An urgent line is available on the Partner plan only. It covers Northwind's working hours and, in addition, 07:00 to 09:00 CET. It is not included on Starter or Studio.

3.5 A Retainer carries a limit on active requests: 1 on Starter, 2 on Studio, and 4 on Partner. Work beyond the limit waits until a request is finished.

## 4. Fees, invoicing, and payment

4.1 The fees below apply to agreements signed or renewed from 1 January 2026. They are exclusive of VAT.

4.2 Retainer fees per month are:

4.2.1 Starter, code NW-RET-S: €2,400, for 24 design hours and 1 active request.

4.2.2 Studio, code NW-RET-M: €5,800, for 64 design hours and 2 active requests.

4.2.3 Partner, code NW-RET-L: €11,200, for 130 design hours and 4 active requests, a second senior designer, and a monthly review call with the studio director. The Partner plan is available from 1 January 2026.

4.3 Fixed-Price Project fees are:

4.3.1 Brand Sprint, code NW-SPR-B: €9,500, over 4 weeks, covering logo, colour, type, a 30-page brand book, and one simple logo animation.

4.3.2 Website Sprint, code NW-SPR-W: €14,000, over 6 weeks, covering up to 8 page templates and a component sheet, and including design QA.

4.3.3 Design System Audit, code NW-AUD-DS: €3,800, over 2 weeks. The audit is a written review and a prioritised list of fixes. It does not include doing the fixes.

4.4 Add-on fees are:

4.4.1 An accessibility audit: €1,200, covering up to 10 screens.

4.4.2 Naming, available on a Brand Sprint only: €2,500, covering up to three name candidates.

4.4.3 Extra handoff calls, or design QA outside a Retainer: €150 per hour.

4.4.4 An extra revision round on a Fixed-Price Project: €450.

4.5 A Retainer prepaid annually in advance receives a discount of 10%. A registered non-profit receives a discount of 15% on Retainers. These discounts never combine. Neither discount applies to a Fixed-Price Project.

4.6 A Retainer is invoiced on the first working day of the month. A Fixed-Price Project is invoiced 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery.

4.7 Payment terms are 14 days from the invoice date. If an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, Northwind pauses work on the account until it is paid. Northwind charges statutory interest on late amounts. Northwind does not charge a separate late fee.

4.8 Dutch clients are charged VAT at 21%. An EU business client established outside the Netherlands is invoiced by reverse charge. VAT treatment follows the Client's place of establishment as a business, not the location of the people attending meetings.

4.9 A change of plan does not rewrite hours already used. On an upgrade, rolled-over hours carry over. On a downgrade, rolled-over hours are capped at the new plan's monthly hours.

## 5. Retainer hours

5.1 The design hours in a Retainer are a monthly budget. They are not a promise of output, and unused hours are not a debt Northwind owes in screens.

5.2 Unused hours roll over to the next month only, and are used first. They then expire. Unused retainer hours are never refunded, including where the Client cancels, pauses, or simply does not send work.

5.3 Hours do not roll for two months, do not roll for the rest of a year, and are not paid out. A longer rollover is not a term of this template.

5.4 The Client will not treat expired hours as a credit against a later invoice.

## 6. Revisions and acceptance

6.1 Two Rounds are included per Deliverable. Before 1 January 2025 the included number was three; under this template it is two.

6.2 The Client gives feedback on a Round within 5 working days. Feedback should be one consolidated set of decisions for that Round.

6.3 If a Round is met with silence for 10 working days, that Round is accepted.

6.4 A further Round on a Fixed-Price Project is an extra round at €450. On a Retainer, further revision time is drawn from the hours.

6.5 Acceptance of a Deliverable, whether express or by the silence in clause 6.3, does not transfer intellectual property before payment. Clause 8 governs ownership.

## 7. Rush

7.1 Rush means a start within 1 working day and a first version within 3 working days. It replaces the standard turnaround in clause 3.2.

7.2 Rush is effective only when the Account Lead confirms it in writing. A message that asks for speed, without that confirmation, is not a rush, and standard turnaround applies.

7.3 The rush fee is a percentage uplift. The percentage in force on the date of the written confirmation applies, including where the work is delivered later. As at the date of this template, that percentage is 25%.

7.4 On a Retainer the uplift is charged in hours. The estimated hours are increased by the percentage in force on the confirmation date. Northwind does not also invoice the same uplift as a separate cash fee on top of the Retainer.

7.5 On a Fixed-Price Project the rush fee is the percentage in clause 7.3. The written confirmation names the work it covers.

## 8. Intellectual property

8.1 The Client owns the Final Files once the fees for those files have been paid in full. Until full payment, Northwind retains the rights. Intellectual property transfers on full payment and not before.

8.2 Drafts, rejected concepts, and explorations that are not part of the Final Files remain Northwind's. Northwind will not reuse a rejected concept as another client's brand.

8.3 Northwind may show finished work in its portfolio after launch, unless the Agreement includes a confidentiality clause. The Client may ask for a confidentiality clause at signing, at no extra cost.

8.4 Nothing in this clause assigns Northwind's pre-existing methods, know-how, or internal tools.

## 9. Confidentiality

9.1 Each party keeps the other's Confidential Information private and uses it only to perform the Agreement. A party may share it with its people who need it for that purpose and who are bound to keep it private.

9.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is public other than by a breach of this clause, information the receiving party already held without a duty of confidence, or information the receiving party must disclose by law. Where the law requires disclosure, the receiving party gives notice when it is legally allowed to do so.

9.3 A confidentiality clause, where the Client asks for one at signing, is free. Where it is in the Agreement, clause 8.3 (portfolio) yields to it.

9.4 The duty in this clause survives the end of the Agreement.

## 10. Non-solicitation

10.1 For 12 months after the engagement ends, the Client will not solicit or hire a Northwind employee who worked on the Client's account. A general advertisement that is not aimed at that person is not solicitation.

10.2 If the Client hires such a person in breach of clause 10.1, a placement fee applies. This Agreement does not state the amount of that fee.

10.3 Clause 10.2 is the remedy Northwind relies on for that hire. It does not limit any other right where the hire also involves misuse of Confidential Information.

## 11. Liability

11.1 Northwind's total liability arising out of this Agreement, in respect of any event or series of connected events, is limited to the fees paid under this Agreement in the previous 12 months.

11.2 Nothing in this clause limits liability that cannot be limited under Dutch law.

11.3 The cap in clause 11.1 is the Client's exclusive financial remedy against Northwind for claims arising out of the Services, subject to clause 11.2.

11.4 Northwind does not warrant that a third party's implementation of the design will meet any standard. Design QA, where it is included, is a review of the design files. It is not a certification of a built product.

## 12. Term and termination

12.1 A Retainer continues month to month until it ends under this clause. A Fixed-Price Project ends when the Deliverables are handed over and the final invoice is issued, unless it is cancelled earlier.

12.2 The Client may cancel a Retainer with 30 days' written notice to the Account Lead. Northwind may cancel a Retainer on the same notice.

12.3 Unused retainer hours are never refunded on cancellation.

12.4 Where a Retainer was prepaid annually, the unused months are refunded minus the discount received for those months. That refund is of unused months of prepayment. It is not a refund of unused hours.

12.5 The Client may cancel a Fixed-Price Project at any time. The kickoff invoice is not refunded. Work done after that invoice, and not yet invoiced, is not charged.

12.6 Figma access is removed 30 days after the end of the engagement. The Client should export files before that removal. Removal of access is not deletion of the files. Clause 14 governs how long files are kept.

12.7 On termination, clauses that by their nature should survive do survive, including clauses 5.2, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14, and 16.

## 13. Pause

13.1 The Client may pause a Retainer once per year, for up to two months.

13.2 The Client gives 14 days' written notice before the next invoice. A pause asked for inside that window takes effect only once the notice period has been met.

13.3 During a pause, Northwind does not invoice the paused months and does not perform the Services, and the support times in clause 3 do not run.

13.4 A pause does not extend the life of unused hours. Rolled-over hours expire at the start of the pause.

## 14. Data retention and hosting

14.1 Client files are hosted in the European Union.

14.2 Northwind keeps project files for 24 months after the last invoice, and then deletes them in the ordinary course. The Client may ask for earlier deletion. Northwind confirms that deletion within 10 working days.

14.3 Invoices and the accounting records that support them are kept for 7 years.

14.4 Deletion of project files does not require Northwind to delete invoices. Removal of Figma access under clause 12.6 does not, by itself, delete the stored files.

14.5 Each party remains responsible for the lawfulness of the personal data it provides. Where a separate data processing arrangement is signed, that arrangement governs processing carried out on the Client's behalf. This clause sets retention and hosting. It does not replace that arrangement.

## 15. Handoff and accessibility

15.1 Northwind provides one handoff call of 45 minutes per Deliverable. Developer questions about that handoff are free for 14 days after the call. After those 14 days, further questions are new work.

15.2 Design tokens are delivered as JSON in the W3C design tokens format.

15.3 The accessibility target for the design is WCAG 2.2 AA. Meeting that target in the file is a design obligation. It is not a certification of the Client's implemented product, and Northwind does not issue such a certification.

15.4 A Website Sprint includes design QA. Design QA outside a Retainer, and extra handoff calls outside a Retainer, are charged at €150 per hour under clause 4.4.3.

15.5 The Client's developers, or a development company the Client contracts directly, implement the work. Northwind's responsibility ends at the design and the handoff described in this clause.

## 16. Governing law and general terms

16.1 This Agreement is governed by Dutch law.

16.2 The courts of Rotterdam have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to any right of appeal that Dutch law gives.

16.3 Notices under this Agreement are in writing, including by email to the Account Lead. A notice is effective when received on a working day, or on the next working day if received on a closed day or outside working hours.

16.4 A change to this Agreement is valid only if it is in writing, including by email where the Account Lead confirms it. A practice of doing something informally does not amend the fee, the hours, the rounds, or the notice periods.

16.5 If a clause is held unenforceable, the rest of the Agreement continues. The affected clause is read down to the extent needed to make it enforceable.

16.6 This Agreement, with its statements of work, is the entire agreement about the Services. It replaces prior discussions on the same subject. It does not exclude liability for fraud, and it does not exclude anything clause 11.2 preserves.

16.7 Neither party assigns the Agreement without the other's written consent, except that Northwind may use its own employees and the tools in clause 2.3. Northwind does not assign the Client's work to a development company.

16.8 A failure or delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of that right.

16.9 This template is issued in English. It is governed by Dutch law whether or not a translation is later prepared. If a translation conflicts with the English text, the English text prevails, and the courts of Rotterdam still hear the dispute.
