# Request brief: Atlas HR onboarding screens

Harbor request, written up 18 November 2025. Atlas HR is an HR-software company on a Studio retainer. This is one request inside that retainer, not a separate project and not a change of plan.

## Background

Atlas asks us to redesign the employee onboarding wizard in their product. The current wizard is a sequence of forms. New hires stall in it, and the Atlas team cannot tell from the screens which step is at fault. They want the same job to feel like a short, guided setup.

Account lead is Sandra Tsapljoek. She filed the request after a call with Atlas. Scope questions go back to her, not into the Figma file as silent additions.

## Goals

Replace the wizard with a clearer sequence. Each step should show where the employee is, what is being asked, and what happens if they stop and come back. Empty, error and default states are in scope. A new product feature is not.

We are designing screens. Atlas's own team, or a development partner they contract themselves, will build them. We do not write the production code for this request.

## Audience

The person in the wizard is a new employee, often on a first morning, often on a laptop they have just been given. They did not buy Atlas. Their employer did. Write and design for someone who is tired and does not know the product's vocabulary.

HR admins configure the wizard, but they are not the audience of these five screens. If a screen only makes sense to an admin, it is the wrong screen.

## Deliverables

Five screens for the employee onboarding wizard, in Figma, with the states listed above. One deliverable, so the revision count is two rounds on the set, not two rounds per screen.

Estimate for the request: 18 hours. That figure is what Sandra will put on the Harbor request for Atlas to approve. Do not start until that approval is there.

## Timeline

Standard retainer turnaround once Atlas approves the estimate: we start within 3 working days, and the first version is within 7 working days. Feedback from Atlas is due within 5 working days of that version. After 10 working days of silence, the round is accepted.

No rush has been asked for. Do not treat the request as rush unless Sandra confirms it in writing.

## Owner

Noor Hassan owns the design. Sandra owns the client relationship and any change to the 18 hours. If the five screens will not fit the estimate, Noor says so before the first version, not after.
