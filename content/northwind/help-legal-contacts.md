# Contracts, NDAs and data requests

Use this page to find the person who handles the request. Your contract is the master services agreement you already have. This article does not repeat it, and it does not quote prices.

## Changing your contract

Want to change something in your contract? Ask your account lead. Say what you want changed. If you have proposed wording, attach it.

Your account lead will not agree the change on the call, and a designer will not either. Changes are handled by our senior lawyer, Vladimir Posjivalov, and signed by the studio director. Vladimir drafts the change. The studio director signs it. Until that signature is on the page, the contract you already have is the one that applies.

Write to your account lead so the request stays with your account. They pass it to Vladimir Posjivalov. Please do not send a marked-up agreement to whoever was in the last design review and ask them to confirm it.

If you are not sure whether something is a contract change or a note about a single deliverable, send it to your account lead anyway and say which you think it is. They will route it. A line in a Harbor request about one screen is a design comment. A line that changes what we owe each other is a contract question.

## NDAs and data protection

NDAs and data protection questions, including deletion requests, go to our lawyer Sofie Milkova. Write to her directly, and copy your account lead if you want them to see it.

If you want files deleted, the request has to reach Sofie. She confirms a deletion in writing. A remark in a meeting, or a comment in a design file, is not the confirmation. Keep her written reply.

If you want us to sign your NDA, send it to Sofie before anyone here signs it. We do not sign an NDA that has only travelled through a project thread, and we do not sign it because a kickoff is close. She reads it first.

Questions about the DPA, and questions about how we handle your data, are the same person. Sofie Milkova. Your account lead can help you describe the project. They do not answer the data question in her place.

## Invoices and VAT

Invoices and VAT are not contract changes, and they are not data requests. Those questions go to Maksim Ryzjikov. He handles invoices and VAT.

Your account lead can tell you what work a month covered. They cannot amend an invoice. If a figure, a name, or a VAT line looks wrong, write to Maksim and say which invoice you mean. The invoice itself sits in Harbor.

## A confidentiality clause

A confidentiality clause can be added free at signing. Tell your account lead while the agreement is being signed, and it is included then.

If the contract is already running and you want the clause added, that is a change to the contract. Ask your account lead. Vladimir Posjivalov handles the change, and the studio director signs it. The clause is not in force because it was discussed. It is in force when the signed change says it is.
