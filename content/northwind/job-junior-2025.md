# Job: junior designer (filled)

This posting is no longer in force. The junior designer role opened in June 2025 and was filled by Yara Haddad in September 2025. Applications are closed. The text below is the posting as it stood when the role was open.

## The role

Northwind Studio is a design agency in Lloydkwartier, Rotterdam. We design for software companies: brand identity, web design, design systems, and product design. We were hiring a junior designer to work in Figma alongside the senior designers, on client work, not on a separate training track.

The studio does not write production code, build mobile apps, run ads, or manage social media. Video, where we do it, is a simple logo animation. A junior here spends their time in the design files.

## How we work

Office days are Tuesday and Thursday at the Rotterdam studio. The other days are remote. Critique is on Wednesday at 14:00, and junior designers bring work in progress like everyone else.

Day to day the tools are Figma, the Harbor client portal, Notion, Linear, Loom, and Google Workspace. Harbor is where client requests live. You would not have been running your own clients on day one. You would have been in the file with someone senior, which is the point of the role.

## Practicalities

Holiday allowance is 27 days, plus Dutch public holidays. The studio closes from 24 December to 2 January. There is one office, in Rotterdam.

This role was filled by Yara Haddad. Please don't apply. The posting is no longer in force.
