# Development partners

Northwind designs. We do not write production code, and we do not build or design native mobile apps. When a project needs engineering, we introduce a development partner and step back. The three studios below are separate companies. We do not subcontract them, we are not responsible for their work, and there are no referral fees.

## Who does what

Pixelforge builds websites. They are in Rotterdam. That is their studio, not a second Northwind office. Northwind's only office is in Lloydkwartier, Rotterdam. Sharing a city does not make Pixelforge part of Northwind.

Byteharbour builds React Native mobile apps. They are in Amsterdam. The Amsterdam office is Byteharbour's, not Northwind's. If a client asks us for an iOS or Android app, the answer is that we don't design or build native mobile apps, and that Byteharbour is the partner we name for React Native.

Lumen Labs builds in Webflow. Use them when the site should be assembled in Webflow rather than handed to a custom front end.

We still do the design either way: brand, web design, design systems, and product design for web apps, in Figma, with tokens as JSON in the W3C design tokens format when the work includes tokens. A Website Sprint includes design QA of the built pages. That QA is us checking against our designs. It is not us fixing the code.

## How the contract works

The client contracts them directly. Northwind is not in that contract, does not invoice their fees, and does not take a margin. A referral fee is not part of the arrangement, in either direction.

What we do provide is the handoff. Each deliverable includes one 45-minute handoff call. Developer questions are free for 14 days after handoff, asked through Harbor. After that, questions are ordinary work: retainer hours, or €150 per hour outside a retainer.

The account lead makes the introduction when the client asks for a builder. After that, scheduling of the build is between the client and the partner.

## What this is not

It is not a joint venture, and it is not a white-label build. If the partner misses a date, that is their project with the client. If our files are wrong, that is ours, and the account lead tells the client. Mixing the two in one status report helps no one.

We also do not run ads, manage social media, or make video beyond a simple logo animation on a Brand Sprint. Those requests are not covered by sending someone to Pixelforge, Byteharbour, or Lumen Labs.
