# Handbook: answering common sales objections

*Internal. Account leads only. Updated 25 February 2026. Do not forward this page, and do not read the discount limits out on a call.*

Four objections come up on almost every intro call. Use the answers below. If a figure is not on this page or the 2026 price list, leave it out and ask Oleksij van Koeropatkin before you write it into a proposal.

## "Too expensive"

Do not drop a number on the call to keep the conversation alive. Put the plan next to hiring, which is the comparison they are already making, and then stop talking.

A retainer is a monthly design budget: named people, a fixed hour allowance, a cap on active requests. Prices below are the 2026 list, excl. VAT.

- Starter (NW-RET-S): €2,400 a month, 24 design hours, 1 active request.
- Studio (NW-RET-M): €5,800 a month, 64 hours, 2 active requests.
- Partner (NW-RET-L): €11,200 a month, 130 hours, 4 active requests, a second senior designer, and a monthly review with Oleksij.

Fixed-price, if they are not looking for a retainer: Brand Sprint €9,500 (4 weeks), Website Sprint €14,000 (6 weeks), Design System Audit €3,800 (2 weeks, a written review, fixes not included).

Hours are a budget, not a promise of a set number of screens. Say that once. Do not invent a salary figure for a hire, and do not invent a day rate. Let them do their own hiring maths.

## "We need code too"

We design for software companies: brand, web, design systems, product design. We do not write production code, build mobile apps, run ads, manage social media, or make video beyond one simple logo animation.

When they need a build, name the partner and stop. The client contracts that studio directly. We do not subcontract them, and there is no referral fee.

- Pixelforge — web, in Rotterdam.
- Byteharbour — React Native mobile apps. Their office is in Amsterdam. That is not a Northwind office.
- Lumen Labs — Webflow.

Handoff from us is one 45-minute call per deliverable, tokens as JSON in the W3C design tokens format, and developer questions free for 14 days after that call.

## "Can we get 3 revision rounds?"

Two rounds are included per deliverable. That has been the rule since 1 January 2025. Do not offer a third round to close the deal.

On a fixed-price project an extra round is €450. On a retainer, a further round is just hours. Feedback is due within 5 working days. After 10 working days of silence, the round is accepted.

If they keep pushing, talk about how they give feedback: one list, one approver. A cleaner first round beats a third round of scattered comments.

## "Discount?"

Account leads may approve up to 5% extra discount on a fixed-price project. Anything above that needs Oleksij before the proposal goes out. This limit is internal. Do not print "up to 5% off" in a deck as if it were a public offer. Approve a specific figure, or say nothing.

Two other discounts exist, and only on retainers. Annual prepayment is 10%. A registered non-profit is 15%. Those two never combine, and neither of them applies to a fixed-price project. Do not stack the 5% on top of another discount.

Agree nothing on the intro call. The only discount that counts is the one written in the proposal.
