# Using your brand book

A Brand Sprint ends with a 30-page brand book. It is the rules for the identity: logo, colour, and type, and how those pieces behave together. It is not a folder of suggestions you outgrow at launch.

The sprint also includes one simple logo animation. That animation is delivered beside the book, not as extra pages inside it. This article is about the book, and about the logo files that go with it.

## What you are meant to use

Use the logo files as they were exported. Do not redraw the mark, re-space the wordmark, or rebuild it from a screenshot because a template felt tight. The book shows the versions you have and the situations they are for.

Clear space is drawn in the book for a reason. Keep other elements outside it: type, photography, another logo, the edge of a page. If a format only works by pulling the logo into that space, the format is wrong, or you need a new asset. Squeezing the mark is not a local edit.

Colour codes are printed in the book. Use those values. Do not sample a colour from a PNG with an eyedropper. Export compression and screen settings will lie to you, and the code in the book will not.

Type is specified in the book as well. The font files themselves are not part of the delivery. You license those in your own name. The book tells you which families we designed for; it does not smuggle the fonts in.

## When the book does not cover it

A social crop, a sponsor lockup, a slide we never drew, an icon the book does not contain: that is a new asset. The 30-page book does not grow on its own after we hand it over.

Open a request in Harbor and say what you need, where it will be used, and which rules in the book it has to respect. On a retainer, the work comes out of your hours. After a Brand Sprint with no retainer, it is new work, quoted on its own. Do not ask a colleague to "just extend the logo" in the meantime. That is how the identity frays, one helpful file at a time.
