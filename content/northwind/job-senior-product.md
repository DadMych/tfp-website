# Job: senior product designer (closed)

This posting is closed and is no longer in force. The posting was closed in June 2026 without a hire; the studio stayed at 14 people. It is kept here so nobody applies against a role that has already ended.

## The role

Northwind Studio is a design agency in Rotterdam. We work with software companies on brand identity, web design, design systems, and product design. This was a senior product designer role: the product work, in Figma, with the account leads and the rest of the design team. We do not write production code, build mobile apps, run advertising, or manage social media.

You would have owned screens from the brief through handoff. Handoff includes one 45-minute call per deliverable, and the files have to be in a state a developer can use. Tokens leave as JSON in the W3C design tokens format. The accessibility target on the work is WCAG 2.2 AA.

## How the week works

The studio is hybrid. Office days are Tuesday and Thursday, at the studio in Lloydkwartier. Other days are remote. Design critique is on Wednesday at 14:00. We use Figma, Harbor, Notion, Linear, Loom, and Google Workspace.

There is one office. It is in Rotterdam. We are not hiring this role in another city.

## Practicalities

The holiday allowance is 27 days, plus Dutch public holidays. The studio is closed from 24 December to 2 January, and on the public holidays. The home-office budget is €600 once per two years.

Do not send an application. The role is closed, nobody was hired, and this posting is no longer in force.
