# Handbook: expenses

File the receipt, name what it was, and do it before the month goes cold. Maksim Ryzjikov cannot reconstruct a receipt from your memory, and he should not have to.

## Receipts

Every expense needs a receipt in the expense tool within 30 days. Photograph it at the table if you have to. A receipt you describe from memory, six weeks later, is not a receipt.

Thirty days is the window. It is not a target to aim past. If the tool will not take the file, say so before the 30 days are gone, not after. No receipt, no reimbursement. We do not keep a second process for people who meant to upload it.

## Working from home

There is a home-office budget of €600, once per two years. It is for setting up the place you actually work on the days you are not in Lloydkwartier. It is not a refresher every January, and it is not a spare €600 on top of a conference.

The same receipt rule applies. The purchase goes in the expense tool, with the receipt, inside 30 days. If you are not sure an item is the home office rather than a hobby, ask before you buy it. Asking afterwards is how a claim gets refused.

## Lunches with clients

Client lunches are allowed. Put the client's name in the note on the expense. "Lunch" is not a note. "Lunch with Brightlane" is. If you cannot name the client, it was not a client lunch.

There is no separate form for this. The receipt and the note are the whole submission. Do not invent a per-person cap in your head and then argue about it. If a lunch looks out of scale, Maksim will ask. You do not need a policy number to know when to be reasonable.

## Conferences and travel to them

Conferences need Oleksij van Koeropatkin's approval before you buy the ticket. "Early bird ends tonight" is not approval. Write to Oleksij with the conference, the dates, and why you should be the person who goes. Wait for the yes.

Travel and lodging for that conference follow the same rule: approved first, receipt in the tool within 30 days afterwards. Do not book a non-refundable fare on a maybe.
