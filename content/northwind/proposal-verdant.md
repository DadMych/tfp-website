# Proposal: Verdant Energy website

Prepared for Verdant Energy. Dated 20 February 2026. Prices exclude VAT.

## Summary

A marketing site for an energy SaaS product: a Website Sprint, plus an accessibility audit of up to 10 screens. The sprint is NW-SPR-W, €14,000, six weeks, up to 8 page templates and a component sheet, with design QA included. The audit is €1,200. Together, €15,200. We design to WCAG 2.2 AA. The audit reviews screens we have designed; it is not a certification of a built site, and we do not write production code.

## Scope

In scope: the site structure, visual direction, up to 8 page templates, a component sheet, design QA against those templates once they are built, and the accessibility audit.

Out of scope: production code, native mobile apps, advertising, social media, and video. Naming is not part of this proposal. A simple logo animation is part of a Brand Sprint, not of this website sprint, and we have not included one.

Two revision rounds are included per deliverable. Feedback within 5 working days; after 10 working days of silence the round is accepted. An extra round is €450.

## Team

Vlas Koerbatski, senior designer, web, leads the work. Eva de Groot, designer, is on the project with him. Kickoff is within 10 working days of signing.

## Timeline (week by week)

- Week 1. Kickoff, sitemap, visual direction.
- Week 2. First templates.
- Week 3. Remaining templates, within the cap of 8.
- Week 4. Component sheet and the first revision round.
- Week 5. Second revision round, and the accessibility audit of up to 10 screens.
- Week 6. Design QA and the handoff call.

One handoff call of 45 minutes is included per deliverable. Developer questions are free for 14 days after handoff. The build itself is not ours. If you want an introduction to a development partner, ask at kickoff; you contract them directly.
