# Harbor changelog

Harbor is the client portal for requests, files, and invoices. Newest entries first. Older behaviour is described in the entry that changed it.

## 15 September 2026

- Changelog checked through this date. No change to request statuses, invoices, or uploads in this entry.

## July 2026

- Loom embeds. A Loom walkthrough can be embedded on a request.

## April 2026

- Upload alerting, added after the outage on 14 April 2026. File uploads failed that day for 3 hours 20 minutes. No files were lost.
- Alerts now fire on upload errors, which they did not do before that outage.

## February 2026

- The request status "In review" replaced the old name "Waiting for client". Same step in the workflow: the studio is waiting on the client.
- Queued, In progress, and Done were not renamed.

## September 2025

- Approvals. A client can approve a deliverable in Harbor.

## June 2025

- Invoices moved into Harbor. A retainer invoice and a project invoice for the account are both filed on the portal from this month.

## May 2025

- Harbor launched. It replaced email-only requests.
- A client submits a request, attaches files, and follows status in the portal. Status names at launch: Queued, In progress, Waiting for client, Done. "Waiting for client" was renamed in February 2026; see that entry.
