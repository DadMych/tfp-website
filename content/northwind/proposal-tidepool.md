# Proposal: Tidepool Payments Partner retainer

From Sandra Tsapljoek, account lead. For Tidepool Payments. Dated 5 March 2026. Prices exclude VAT.

## Summary

A Partner retainer: NW-RET-L, €11,200 a month, 130 hours, 4 active requests. The plan includes a second senior designer and a monthly review call with Oleksij van Koeropatkin, the studio director. First reply within 4 working hours. The urgent line is included, during working hours and from 07:00 to 09:00 CET, for launch blockers.

Paid annually in advance, the retainer is 10% off: €120,960 per year. The discount applies to the retainer only and does not combine with any other discount.

## Scope

Hours are a budget for product design on your web app: flows, screens, and the components those screens need. We do not write production code and we do not design or build native mobile apps.

Two revision rounds are included per deliverable. Unused hours roll over one month and then expire. They are never refunded. A rush, confirmed by me in writing, starts within 1 working day with a first version within 3 working days. The rush fee in force now is 40%. On the retainer that is charged in hours: a 10-hour rush uses 14 hours.

## Team

I remain the account lead. Tomás Pereira, senior designer, product, is the second senior on the account. Oleksij joins the monthly review. Working hours are 09:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday.

## Timeline (week by week)

The retainer is monthly, not a six-week project. The first month, week by week:

- Week 1. Kickoff, Harbor access, Figma editor access, first requests in.
- Week 2. First versions on the open requests, up to 4 active at once.
- Week 3. Revisions on what has come back.
- Week 4. Monthly review with Oleksij van Koeropatkin. Unused hours from this month roll over into the next, and only into the next.

After that, the month repeats: 130 hours, the same team, the review call. Kickoff is within 10 working days of signing.

## Investment

| Option | What you pay (excl. VAT) | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Partner, monthly | €11,200 per month | 130 hours |
| Partner, annual prepayment, 10% off | €120,960 per year | 130 hours each month |

Monthly retainers are invoiced on the first working day of the month. Payment terms are 14 days. An invoice more than 30 days overdue pauses the work. We charge statutory interest and no separate late fee.

## Terms

Cancel with 30 days' written notice to me. If you have prepaid annually, unused months are refunded minus the discount you received for them. Unused hours inside a month are not refunded. You own final files once they are paid for. This proposal includes a confidentiality clause at no charge, so we will not put the work in the portfolio. Figma access comes off 30 days after the engagement ends. Editor access is part of Partner while the retainer runs; it is not a separate fee.

Dutch clients pay 21% VAT. An EU business client outside the Netherlands is reverse charge. We will confirm which applies on the first invoice, from the contracting entity.
