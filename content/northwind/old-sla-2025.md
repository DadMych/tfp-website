# Support and response times (2025, superseded)

Superseded. This schedule is no longer in force. It described support from 8 January 2025 until it was replaced on 1 January 2026. Do not use it to answer a client who is asking about current reply times.

## Working day

In this schedule, a working day is a day the studio is open: Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 CET, excluding the closure from 24 December through 2 January and excluding Dutch public holidays. A message that arrives outside those hours waits until the next working day before the clock starts. That definition of the working day is background. What this page gets retired for is the reply table below.

## Reply times in 2025

| Plan | First reply | Urgent line |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | within 2 working days | no |
| Studio | within 1 working day | no |

There was no Partner plan in 2025, and this schedule has no urgent line for anyone. Studio's commitment here is a first reply within 1 working day, not a same-day reply. Starter is within 2 working days. Fixed-price work was not given a separate row on this page.

## Why this page is closed

Superseded on 1 January 2026, when the support schedule changed. Partner, the urgent line, and a different Studio reply time all sit in the current schedule, not in this one. Quoting "within 1 working day" from this page, as if it were still the Studio promise, is a mistake.

This document is archived. It is no longer in force. If an old statement of work points at "the support schedule", read the date on that statement of work before you read this file.
