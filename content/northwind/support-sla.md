# Support and response times

Working hours are 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time, Monday to Friday.

## Response times by plan

| Plan | First reply | Urgent line | Monthly review call |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter (NW-RET-S) | within 2 working days | no | no |
| Studio (NW-RET-M) | same working day | no | no |
| Partner (NW-RET-L) | within 4 working hours | yes | yes |

Fixed-price projects get same-working-day replies during the project and for 14 days after delivery.

## Urgent line

The urgent line is for Partner clients only. It is a phone number shared at onboarding, answered during working hours plus 07:00 to 09:00 CET. It is for problems that block a launch, not for new requests.

## Where to ask

Use the client portal for requests and questions so the whole team sees them. Email to your account lead also works. We don't offer support through WhatsApp or other chat apps.
