# Upgrading or downgrading your plan

You can move between Starter, Studio and Partner. The move is not instant, and the hours you have already rolled over do not behave the same way in both directions. Write to your account lead and say which plan you want. They will confirm the date it takes effect.

These are the 2026 plans. Prices exclude VAT.

- Starter, NW-RET-S: €2,400 a month, 24 design hours, 1 active request. First reply within 2 working days.
- Studio, NW-RET-M: €5,800 a month, 64 hours, 2 active requests. First reply the same working day.
- Partner, NW-RET-L: €11,200 a month, 130 hours, 4 active requests. A second senior designer, a monthly review with the studio director, first reply within 4 working hours, and the urgent line during working hours plus 07:00–09:00 CET.

If your current agreement is still on 2025 pricing, say so when you write. Your account lead will tell you which price applies to the change. Do not assume the figure on an old invoice is the figure on the next one.

## Upgrading

An upgrade applies from the next invoice. You are not billed the higher plan for the month already invoiced. From that next invoice you have the new plan's hours, its active-request cap, and, if you are moving to Partner, the things only Partner includes.

Rolled-over hours carry over when you upgrade. Unused hours already roll one month and then expire; an upgrade does not wipe the month you are carrying, and it does not extend that month into a second one.

## Downgrading

A downgrade takes effect after 30 days' notice. Until that notice runs, you stay on the plan you have, including its hours and its active requests.

On a downgrade, rolled-over hours are capped to the new plan's monthly hours. Starter's month is 24 hours, Studio's is 64, Partner's is 130. If you leave Studio for Starter, anything you were carrying is capped at 24. The cap is the new month's size. We do not pay out the difference, and we do not park the excess for later. Hours above the cap are gone. Unused hours are never refunded in any case.

Partner's second senior designer, the monthly review with the studio director, and the urgent line belong to Partner. They do not come with you onto Studio or Starter.

## Annual prepayment

If you paid the year up front, do not change plan and assume the arithmetic will sort itself out on the next invoice. Email your account lead first. The annual discount is 10% on a retainer, and it does not combine with the non-profit discount. Which price you are actually on is a conversation, not a guess in the portal.
