Unused hours are never refunded, including these. If you have work ready in April, send it through Harbor and we will use the rolled hours first. One active request at a time on Starter, same as always.

If you already know April will also be quiet, tell me before the next invoice and we can look at a pause instead. A pause is once a year, up to two months, and it needs 14 days' notice before the invoice. It is not a way to keep March hours alive past April.
