# Handoff to developers

## What developers get

Every handoff includes the Figma file with all states (hover, focus, empty, error), a spacing and type sheet, exported assets in SVG and PNG, and design tokens as a JSON file in the W3C design tokens format.

## Handoff call

We run one 45-minute handoff call with your developers per deliverable. Further calls come out of retainer hours, or cost €150 per hour on fixed-price projects.

## After handoff

During the 14 days after handoff we answer developer questions in the portal at no charge. After that, questions are billed as normal work.

## Design QA

We can review the built pages against the designs before launch. Design QA is included in the Website Sprint (NW-SPR-W). For anything else it is billed from retainer hours or at €150 per hour.
