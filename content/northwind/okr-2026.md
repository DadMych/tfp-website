# Studio goals 2026

Oleksij van Koeropatkin. Notes from 8 January 2026. Internal. Four goals for the year. No revenue target on this page. If a goal is not listed here, it is not a studio goal.

## Sign 3 Partner retainers

The Partner plan (NW-RET-L) started on 1 January 2026. €11,200 a month, 130 hours, 4 active requests, a second senior designer, a monthly review with me, first reply within 4 working hours, and the urgent line.

The goal is 3 Partner retainers signed in 2026. Kestrel Analytics is the first, account lead Sandra Tsapljoek, on from January. A fixed-price project does not count. A Studio client who has not signed Partner does not count.

Vladislav Boerdonos and Sandra own the conversations. I join the monthly review once they are on the plan, not before they have signed. We do not discount Partner below the rules already in the sales handbook to hit this number.

## Satisfaction above 4.5

The 2025 client survey came back at 4.6 out of 5, from 22 responses. The 2026 goal is to stay above 4.5 on the same kind of survey. We do not run a second score.

Account leads own the day-to-day. Vladislava owns the survey when we run it again. A single angry thread is not a failure of the goal. The survey is.

## Rush stays at or under 15% of requests

No more than 15% of requests should be rush. A request counts as rush only when the account lead has confirmed rush in writing. The fee in force on that confirmation date is the one we charge. I am not setting a new fee on this page.

Maksim Ryzjikov counts this from Harbor. Account leads check capacity with me before they confirm a rush. Designers are planned at 26 billable hours a week, and Partner clients keep scheduling priority. If rush starts crowding the week, we say no, even if the client will pay the fee.

## Six posts on the studio site

Publish 6 blog posts in 2026. Posts already live from 2025 do not count toward the six. No list of titles on this page. A post does not announce a price or a policy of its own.
