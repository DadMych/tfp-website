# Handbook: welcome to Northwind

You are at a design studio of 16 people, founded in 2017 by Oleksij van Koeropatkin. One office, in Lloydkwartier, Rotterdam. Clients are often somewhere else in Europe, or in North America. We are not.

We do brand identity, web design, design systems and product design, for software companies. We do not write production code, build mobile apps, run ads, manage social media, or make video beyond a simple logo animation. If a client asks, the answer is no, said early.

## Where you are expected

Office days are Tuesday and Thursday, in Lloydkwartier. The rest of the week is hybrid. Working hours are 09:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday. The studio is closed on Dutch public holidays and from 24 December through 2 January.

## Tools

You will be given Google Workspace. The internal wiki is Notion, which is where you are reading this. Internal tasks go in Linear. Design work is Figma. Walkthroughs that do not need a meeting are Loom. Clients send requests, files and invoices through Harbor, the portal we launched in May 2025. Do not start work from a private inbox and a private file.

## First week

- Get into Google Workspace, Notion, Figma, Linear, Loom and Harbor. If a login is missing, tell Maksim. Do not wait for a full set of logins to introduce yourself.
- Read this handbook far enough to know who does invoices and who leads an account.
- Come in on Tuesday and Thursday. Learn the walk from the station to Lloydkwartier once, in daylight.
- Sit with the work, not only with the calendar. Ask a designer if you can watch a file before you open one of your own.
- You have 27 holiday days plus Dutch public holidays. The home-office budget is €600 once per two years. Both have their own pages. Do not guess the rules from a colleague's memory.
