# Studio closures 2026

The studio is closed on the days below. Working hours on open days are 09:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday. A request sent on a closed day starts the next working day. This list was issued on 1 December 2025 for the year ahead.

| Date | Weekday | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January | Thursday | New Year's Day. Studio closure |
| 2 January | Friday | Studio closure. The year-end shutdown from the previous December runs through this Friday |
| 3 April | Friday | Good Friday |
| 6 April | Monday | Easter Monday |
| 27 April | Monday | King's Day |
| 5 May | Tuesday | Liberation Day |
| 14 May | Thursday | Ascension Day |
| 25 May | Monday | Whit Monday |
| 24–31 December | Thursday 24 December through Thursday 31 December | Year-end closure |

The standing rule is that the studio is closed from 24 December through 2 January, not only on the public holidays inside that stretch. The December row is the 2026 end of that closure; it continues through 2 January, which falls in the next year and is the same standing rule, not a separate holiday.

Weekends are outside working hours all year. They are not listed again here. Liberation Day is on this list, so the studio is closed on 5 May.

Harbor accepts a request on a closed day, and the request waits. The clock for a start (within 3 working days on a retainer, or within 1 working day for a confirmed rush) starts on the next working day. The first working day after 2 January 2026 is the Monday that follows that Friday, because the weekend sits in between. After 31 December the closure is still running under the standing rule through 2 January, so a request sent in the last week of December does not start on a day inside that closure.
