# DRAFT: 2026 pricing proposal (not adopted)

This proposal was rejected at the pricing meeting of 4 November 2025. It was not adopted. It is no longer in force. Do not quote it to a client, and do not use it in a renewal.

The text under "The proposal" is what was circulated on 15 October 2025. The prices in it are not the 2026 prices.

## The proposal

From Vladislava Wolinska, operations and finance. Draft for discussion, mid-October 2025, ahead of a 2026 price decision.

I propose three changes for agreements from 1 January 2026:

- Studio retainer at €6,200 per month.
- A Partner retainer at €12,000 per month.
- Remove the annual prepayment discount. In 2025 that discount is 15% for a retainer paid annually in advance. This draft would drop it, not reduce it.

I am not, in this draft, restating Starter or the fixed-price projects. The decision we need first is the Studio figure, whether we add a Partner plan at the figure above, and whether the annual discount stays.

Reason for bringing it now: Q4 capacity is tight, and a higher Studio price plus a larger plan was the shape I wanted the meeting to argue about. It is a proposal, not a quote.

## What happened to it

Rejected. The pricing meeting of 4 November 2025 did not adopt €6,200, did not adopt €12,000, and did not remove the annual discount.

The meeting chose other figures, in force from 1 January 2026: Starter €2,400, Studio €5,800, Partner €11,200, Brand Sprint €9,500, Website Sprint €14,000, and the annual prepayment discount moving from 15% to 10%. Those are the prices that count. This draft is archived.
