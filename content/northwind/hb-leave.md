# Handbook: holidays and leave

You have 27 holiday days a year. Dutch public holidays sit on top of that. They are not deducted from the 27, and they are not a bank you can move because a public holiday fell on a weekend you had plans for. The studio closes for them.

## The Christmas shutdown is not your holiday

The studio is closed from 24 December through 2 January. Those days are studio days off. They do not come out of your 27. You do not book them as leave, and you do not "save" them by offering to work. The office is shut. Clients are told the same dates.

Do not schedule a kickoff, a handoff or a crit into that closure. Requests that arrive while we are shut start on the next working day.

## Booking

Book leave in Google Calendar, so the people staffing the week can see it without asking you. Then tell your account lead two weeks ahead. Both, not one of them. A calendar block nobody was told about is how a client review gets booked onto your day off. A message to your account lead, with nothing on the calendar, is how the next person plans you in anyway.

Two weeks is the notice. If the days include a Tuesday or a Thursday, say so, because those are office days and someone may be counting on you in Lloydkwartier. If you are on a client that cannot slip, talk to the account lead before you buy the travel. Putting it on the calendar does not move the client's deadline.

## What this page does not decide

This page is your 27 days, the public holidays, and the Christmas closure. It is not a sick-leave policy, and it is not a procedure for unpaid time. Do not copy a rule from another employer into the calendar and assume we share it. If the situation is not holiday, ask Maksim Ryzjikov before you announce the dates to a client.
