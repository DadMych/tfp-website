# Handbook: contracts and legal questions

Internal. This page is who you ask. It is not a copy of the master services agreement. The clause text, the numbers, and anything a client would hold you to live in the MSA. If you cannot point at that document, you do not have an answer yet.

## Contracts and changes

Vladimir Posjivalov is the senior lawyer. He owns the master services agreement and the SOW templates. He owns every contract change a client asks for, whether they call it a tweak, a clarification, or "just this once".

You can hear the request. You can write it down and send it to him. You cannot agree it. Account leads never agree contract changes themselves. Vladislav Boerdonos and Sandra Tsapljoek pass the request on. Designers do the same. A yes on a kickoff call, a Harbor comment, or a Slack thread is not a change to the contract. It is a sentence Vladimir then has to walk back.

He drafts the change, and the studio director signs. Oleksij van Koeropatkin is the studio director. Nobody else signs a contract, an SOW, or a change to either. Not the account lead who has the relationship, and not the person who happened to have the PDF open.

Questions about IP, and questions about non-solicitation, go to Vladimir as well. Tell the client you will ask him. Do not recite a clause from memory in the reply.

## The DPA, deletion, and NDAs

Sofie Milkova is the lawyer for the DPA and for data protection questions. A client who asks where files sit, who can see them, or what we delete, is asking her.

File deletion requests go to Sofie. She confirms them in writing. A "will do" in a meeting, or a reaction on a Harbor comment, is not that confirmation. Wait for her note before you tell the client it is done, and before you tell a colleague it is done.

NDAs are hers. A client NDA goes to Sofie before anyone signs it. If their counsel sends an NDA to you, or to a designer, forward it to her and leave it unsigned. Length is not a reason to sign. A kickoff date is not a reason to sign. She reads it first.

## Confidentiality clauses

A confidentiality clause is free at signing. If the client wants one, say so while the agreement is still being signed, and Vladimir puts it in. Do not leave it as a verbal extra that someone will "add to the page later".

Adding one to a contract that is already running is a contract change. Same path as any other change: Vladimir Posjivalov drafts it, and the studio director signs. You do not attach it yourself because the client asked nicely.

## What you do not improvise

When a client asks what the contract says, send them to the MSA or to Vladimir. Do not type a shorter version into a proposal because the real clause felt long. Do not ask Vladislava Wolinska. Press is her job. Contracts are not.

Sofie and Vladimir are not on a client's design team. Do not put them in Figma "so legal can see the screens" and then treat that as a review of the work. If they need to see something to answer a question, the account lead sends it to them.
