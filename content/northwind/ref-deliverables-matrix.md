# What each plan and project delivers

What is included in the fee. Prices are excluding VAT and match the 2026 rate card. This matrix is dated 12 January 2026.

| Offer | Typical deliverables | Revision rounds | Handoff calls | Figma access | Design QA | Urgent line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter, NW-RET-S, €2,400/month | Ongoing design, 24 hours a month, 1 active request. First reply within 2 working days | 2 per deliverable | 1 per deliverable, 45 minutes | viewer | Not in the fee | No |
| Studio, NW-RET-M, €5,800/month | Ongoing design, 64 hours a month, 2 active requests. First reply the same working day | 2 per deliverable | 1 per deliverable, 45 minutes | viewer | Not in the fee | No |
| Partner, NW-RET-L, €11,200/month | Ongoing design, 130 hours a month, 4 active requests, a second senior designer, a monthly review call with the studio director. First reply within 4 working hours | 2 per deliverable | 1 per deliverable, 45 minutes | editor | Not in the fee | Yes. Working hours plus 07:00–09:00 CET |
| Brand Sprint, NW-SPR-B, €9,500 | 4 weeks. Logo, colour, type, a 30-page brand book, one simple logo animation | 2 per deliverable | 1 per deliverable, 45 minutes | viewer | Not in the fee | No |
| Website Sprint, NW-SPR-W, €14,000 | 6 weeks. Up to 8 page templates and a component sheet | 2 per deliverable | 1 per deliverable, 45 minutes | viewer | Included. Website Sprint only | No |
| Design System Audit, NW-AUD-DS, €3,800 | 2 weeks. A written review and a prioritised list of fixes. Doing the fixes is not part of the audit | 2 per deliverable | 1 per deliverable, 45 minutes | viewer | Not in the fee | No |

Figma access is viewer on every row except Partner, where it is editor. Editor access is not added to Starter, Studio, or a fixed-price project by asking in a meeting; Partner is the plan that includes it.

Design QA in the fee is Website Sprint only. On a retainer it uses hours. Outside a retainer, and on a fixed-price project that is not a Website Sprint, it is €150 per hour, the same rate as an extra handoff call.

Add-ons, not rows: an accessibility audit is €1,200 for up to 10 screens. Naming is €2,500, up to three name candidates, and only on a Brand Sprint. An extra revision round on a fixed-price project is €450.

The urgent line is Partner only. It is for problems that block a launch. It is not a way to open a new request.
