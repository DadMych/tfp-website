Yes. A retainer can be paused once per year, for up to two months. July and August uses that once-a-year pause in full. You would not be able to pause again until next year.

Notice has to reach me at least 14 days before the next invoice. Retainers are invoiced on the first working day of the month, so the July invoice is the one this pause has to beat. Reply to confirm and I will mark July and August as paused, and the retainer picks up with the September invoice.

Please read this part carefully, because it is different from a rollover. This is a pause, not extra time to use old hours. Rolled-over hours expire at the start of the pause. Hours also do not accrue while you are paused, and unused hours are not refunded.

Your Starter terms otherwise stay as they are. Same plan when you come back, unless you write to change it.
