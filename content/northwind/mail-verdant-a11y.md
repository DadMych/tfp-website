From: Vlas Koerbatski

Date: 4 June 2026
Subject: Re: Can you certify the site?

Hello,

We can't certify the site. We don't write production code, so we can't sign a claim about the site that gets built from the files. A certificate would be about the implementation, and the implementation isn't ours.

What we do is design to WCAG 2.2 AA. That's the target on the pages: contrast, focus, the states a screen needs before it goes to handoff. The Website Sprint also includes design QA, which is a check of the design, not a legal certification and not a test of the shipped code.

If you want a written pass over screens, the accessibility audit is the add-on. It's €1,200, and it covers up to 10 screens. You get notes on where the design meets that target and where it doesn't. You don't get a certificate, and you don't get us fixing the code afterwards.

Say if you want the audit added. I'll keep the page work moving either way.

Vlas Koerbatski
Senior designer, Northwind Studio
