# Plans and prices (2026)

These prices apply to all agreements signed or renewed from 1 January 2026. Prices exclude VAT.

## Retainers

Retainers are monthly. Hours are a budget, not a promise of output.

| Plan | Code | Price per month | Design hours | Active requests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | NW-RET-S | €2,400 | 24 | 1 at a time |
| Studio | NW-RET-M | €5,800 | 64 | 2 at a time |
| Partner | NW-RET-L | €11,200 | 130 | 4 at a time |

The Partner plan includes a second senior designer and a monthly review call with the studio director.

## Fixed-price projects

| Project | Code | Price | Typical length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand Sprint | NW-SPR-B | €9,500 | 4 weeks |
| Website Sprint | NW-SPR-W | €14,000 | 6 weeks |
| Design System Audit | NW-AUD-DS | €3,800 | 2 weeks |

NW-SPR-B covers logo, colour, type, a 30-page brand book and one simple logo animation. NW-SPR-W covers up to 8 page templates and a component sheet. NW-AUD-DS is a written review of an existing Figma library with a prioritised list of fixes; it does not include doing the fixes.

## Annual discount

Retainers paid annually in advance get 10% off. The discount does not apply to fixed-price projects and cannot be combined with any other discount.
