# Figma access

The work lives in Figma. How far you can go inside a file depends on the seat we give you, and seats are not passed around by forwarding a link.

## Viewer, unless you are on Partner

You get viewer access by default. A viewer can open the file, follow the work, and export. A viewer does not edit the source.

Only the Partner plan includes editor access. An editor can change the file. Starter and Studio stay on viewer seats even when someone on your team is a designer too.

Do not treat a comment in the file as a substitute for the request in Harbor. The request is the record. The file is the work.

## Asking for a teammate

New people do not get access because a colleague already has it. Request them through Harbor. Name the person and say whether they need viewer access or, on Partner only, editor access. Tell us when they actually need it. We add the seat to the client project, not to a personal file someone duplicated.

If they leave your company, say so in Harbor as well. A seat you no longer recognise is still a seat.

## When the engagement ends

Figma access is removed 30 days after the end of the engagement. That includes viewers and editors. The 30 days are there so you can export. They are not an extra month of design.

Download what you need before that date. Final files are yours once they are paid for. After the seats are gone, you no longer have a way into the file. We still keep files for 24 months after the last invoice, but that is not a seat, and it is not a reason to wait.

## How to export

Open the file and export the frames you need. Logos leave as SVG, PNG or PDF. Icons leave as SVG. A simple logo animation, when the project includes one, leaves as MP4 and as Lottie. The Figma file itself is the source; exports are the copies other people can place without editing our working file.

If a frame will not export cleanly, say so on the Harbor request while you still have access. Do not wait out the 30 days and then ask us to recover a seat.
