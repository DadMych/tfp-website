# Fonts and licences

We specify type. We do not sell it, and we do not lend you ours. The fonts in a brand are licensed by you, in your own name, from the foundry.

## What we do during a Brand Sprint

On a Brand Sprint we recommend foundries and families that fit the identity. We also estimate what the licences are likely to cost, so you can budget before you fall in love with a specimen. That estimate is a planning figure. It is not a Northwind invoice, and it is not a quote from the foundry.

We never resell fonts. We will not buy a licence in Northwind's name and pass the files on. We will not drop font files into the delivery folder as a convenience. If the files are not licensed to you, they do not belong in your brand kit.

If you already own a family and want us to design with it, say so at kickoff. We can work with it when it actually fits. Naming it in week three, after the system is drawn, is a different conversation.

## Desktop is not a web font licence

A desktop licence is what lets people install the font on computers. A web font licence is what lets you serve the font on a website. They are separate purchases. Buying one does not cover the other.

Tell us which of those you intend to buy. A marketing site, a product, and a slide deck do not draw on the same licence. We will design for the use you are actually licensing. We will not assume a desktop file can be uploaded to your site because the letters look the same.

## What arrives from us

The brand book names the families and shows them in use. The Figma source uses them in the file. Neither of those is a licence, and neither is a font file you can install. Your foundry account, in your own name, is where the licence lives. If a developer or a printer asks us to "just send the fonts", we will send them back to you.
