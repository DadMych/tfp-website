# Plans and prices (2024, archived)

**Archived. No longer in force.** These are the prices Northwind published for 2024. Do not quote them on a new agreement, a renewal, or a change of plan. They are here so an old contract can be read against the list that applied when it was signed.

Prices below exclude VAT. There was no Partner plan.

## Retainers

| Plan | Price per month |
|---|---|
| Starter | €1,900 |
| Studio | €4,800 |

This page does not record an hour allowance or an active-request cap for 2024. Do not copy the later allowances back onto a 2024 agreement.

## Fixed-price

| Project | Price |
|---|---|
| Brand Sprint | €7,900 |

A Website Sprint price is not on this list. Do not fill one in.

## Annual discount

A retainer paid annually in advance received 15% off. The discount applied to retainers. It is part of this archived list, not a discount you can offer on a 2026 agreement. The annual discount in force now is a different figure, on the current price list.

## What else was different

Before 1 January 2025, three rounds of revisions were included per deliverable. That rule ended when the two-round policy started. It is not revived by anyone finding this page.

Agreements signed on these 2024 prices stayed on them until they were renewed. A renewal uses the price list in force at renewal, not this one.
