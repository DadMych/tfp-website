# Rush work and scheduling

## Standard turnaround

A request on a retainer is started within 3 working days and a first version is delivered within 7 working days. Fixed-price projects follow the schedule agreed at kickoff.

## Rush

Rush means starting within 1 working day and delivering a first version within 3. Rush is available on all plans, depending on capacity, and must be confirmed in writing by your account lead before it applies.

From 1 March 2026 the rush fee is 40% on top of the normal cost. Before that date it was 25%. On retainers the fee is charged in hours: a 10-hour rush request uses 14 hours. On fixed-price projects it is charged as 40% of the cost of the rushed part, and extra revision rounds during a rush are charged with the same 40% added.

## Holidays

The studio is closed between 24 December and 2 January, and on Dutch public holidays. Requests sent during a closure start on the first working day after it.
