From: Vladislava Wolinska
To: Open Data Collective
Date: 30 March 2026
Subject: Re: Non-profit discount and annual prepayment

Hello,

You can have one. They cannot be combined.

The non-profit discount is 15% on retainers, for a registered non-profit. It does not apply to fixed-price projects. The annual discount is 10%, and it applies when a retainer is prepaid for the year. It also does not apply to fixed-price work.

Taking both would stack the two. We don't do that. You choose. If you are eligible for the non-profit rate, that is 15% off the retainer. If you would rather prepay the year and take the annual rate instead, that is 10%. You don't get 15% and then another 10% off the remainder.

I won't pick the plan for you in this email. Whichever retainer you settle on, the same rule holds: one discount, on the retainer, and the two rates cannot be combined.

Vladislava Wolinska
Operations and finance, Northwind Studio
