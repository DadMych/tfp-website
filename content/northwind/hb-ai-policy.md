# Handbook: using AI tools

A public tool is any chatbot or image generator where the prompt leaves our accounts. If you would not paste the text into a stranger's document, you do not paste it there. Michiel Dembitski owns the doubtful cases. If you are unsure, ask Michiel before you paste, not after you have a result you quite like.

## What does not go in

Client files do not go into public AI tools. Neither do confidential briefs, transcripts of their calls, exports of their product, or a "shortened" version you tell yourself is harmless. Shortened is still their information.

That includes the clients who have a confidentiality clause, Tidepool Payments and Halden Health, and it includes everyone else. The ban is not limited to those two. Those two are simply the ones where a slip is also a clause we signed. Their files are not a candidate for "just this once".

Deleting the chat afterwards does not undo it. Do not paste, then tidy up.

## What is allowed

Figma's built-in AI features are approved on non-confidential files. That means our own scratch work, and client files that are not under a confidentiality clause, and only inside Figma. It does not mean exporting a Tidepool or Halden frame so you can run it through something else. It does not mean a public website with a prompt box.

If the file is confidential, you do not use the built-in features on it either. Non-confidential is the boundary. When you cannot tell, ask Michiel. Waiting a day is cheaper than explaining a prompt.

## Images

AI-generated imagery goes into client work only when the client has approved that use, before you place it. Approval after they have seen it in a deck and felt awkward is not the approval.

When it ships, it is labelled. In the file, and in the note that goes with the delivery, say that the image was generated. Unlabelled generated images are a breach of this page, even if the picture is good and even if the client "probably wouldn't mind". Stock photography in a mockup is a placeholder until they buy a licence. A generated image is not a clever way around that.

## If you are about to argue with this page

Ask Michiel. Bring the file name and what you wanted the tool to do. Do not bring a finished output and a request to bless it. The answer may be no. No is a complete answer.
