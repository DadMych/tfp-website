# Onboarding

## Before kickoff

After signing we send a short intake form. Please return it with your brand assets (logos, fonts, existing guidelines), access to your current Figma files if you have any, and the names of the people who approve work on your side. Approval works best with one person.

## Week by week

- **Week 1:** kickoff call (60 minutes), we review what you have and agree priorities.
- **Week 2:** first direction or first request delivered.
- **Week 3 onwards:** regular rhythm. Retainers get a weekly written update every Friday.

## Tools

We work in Figma and share everything through the client portal. You get viewer access to our Figma project for your account; editor access is available on the Partner plan. We use Loom for walkthrough videos.

## Your first invoice

The first retainer invoice is sent at kickoff and covers the first month in full, regardless of the start date within the month.
