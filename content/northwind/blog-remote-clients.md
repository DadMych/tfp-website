# Blog: working with a Rotterdam studio from Toronto

I'm Vladislav Boerdonos. I run accounts at Northwind. Many of our clients are elsewhere in Europe or in North America. If you are in Toronto, the distance is a specific kind of far. Not so far that a call is impossible. Far enough that a bad habit of meetings will ruin both of our days.

## One office, and a window that works for both

We have one studio, in Lloydkwartier, Rotterdam. Working hours are 09:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday. We are not going to pretend we have a desk in your time zone. What we do have is a standing window for North American clients: 16:00–18:00 CET.

On a day like this one, late March, that window is late morning into early afternoon in Toronto. It is the end of our day and the middle of yours. It is enough for a decision. It is not enough for a workshop, and I will not book it as one. If the thing you wanted from the call was a tour of the file, that tour should have been a recording.

## Loom first, call second

A Loom walkthrough covers most of what a status meeting used to cover. I can talk through a flow once, you can watch it when your morning actually starts, and your notes can come back in one pass instead of as interruptions while I'm still talking. We record those on purpose. A call is for the point we can't settle in the recording: a real disagreement, a choice between two directions, something that needs a face.

Kickoff is the exception I will still put in the diary. After that, the default is async. You do not need to see us to approve a screen. You need the screen, a way to comment, and a person who reads the comments.

## The work should not be waiting on the overlap

Requests go through Harbor, not through a call and not through a chain of email that only one of us can find. You can file one while we're asleep. We pick it up inside the working day. The overlap between Toronto and Rotterdam is a tool for decisions, not the pipe the work has to fit through.

If you only talk to us at 16:00, you will think we are a studio that meets. We're a studio that designs, and meets when the recording isn't enough. Put the request in Harbor, watch the Loom, and save the late slot for the question that actually needs both of us awake and paying attention.

## We stop when the studio stops

18:00 CET is the end of the day, even if your afternoon still has room in it. We will not add a second call after that. The hours after our window are for you to watch the recording and write one set of notes. Feedback that comes back within 5 working days, as one list, keeps the work moving. A standing meeting that exists because the calendar had a gap does not. Use 16:00 when a decision is stuck. Use Harbor for everything else.
