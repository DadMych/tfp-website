# Revisions policy

## What's included

Every deliverable includes two rounds of revisions. A round is one consolidated list of feedback from you, sent in one message or one Figma comment thread, followed by our updated version.

## What counts as a revision

Changes to the agreed direction count as revisions: colours, copy placement, layout tweaks, swapping an image. A change of brief does not: a new audience, a new page, or a new deliverable is new work and is quoted separately, or comes out of your retainer hours.

Fixing our own mistakes never counts as a round.

## Extra rounds

A third or later round costs €450 per round on fixed-price projects. On retainers, extra rounds simply use retainer hours, at no extra charge beyond the hours.

## Feedback timing

Please send feedback within 5 working days. If we don't hear back within 10 working days, the round is treated as accepted and we move on.
