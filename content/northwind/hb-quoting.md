# Handbook: how to quote a request

*Internal. For designers and account leads. Updated 19 January 2026.*

A request does not start when someone is excited about it. It starts when the client has approved an estimate in Harbor. This page is how that estimate is made.

## Read it once

Open the Harbor request. Check which plan the client is on, how many active requests they already have, and whether this is a new deliverable or a revision on one we have already handed over.

Active requests at a time: Starter 1, Studio 2, Partner 4. If they are already at the cap, the new request waits. Do not quietly open another one because the work looks small.

Partner clients get scheduling priority. That affects when you can start. It does not change the estimate.

## Estimate in hours

Write the estimate in hours, not in screens and not in days. Break it into the pieces you can actually point at: frames, states, a handoff. If you cannot name the piece, it is not in the estimate yet.

Designers are planned at 26 billable hours per week. An estimate that only fits if someone works past that needs the account lead to look at the week before it is sent. Do not discover it on the day you meant to start.

## Add a 15% buffer

Take the raw hour figure and add a 15% buffer before the client sees it. The buffer covers the questions that always show up after the first look. It is not a padding you mention, and it is not a second quote you can waive to be nice.

Share one number in Harbor: the estimate including the 15% buffer. Do not show the raw figure and the buffer as two lines. The client approves one estimate.

## Get the yes, then start

Put the estimate on the Harbor request and wait. The client approves it there. Until that approval is in, the request stays queued. Starting "so we do not lose the week" is how hours get spent that nobody agreed.

Standard turnaround, once they have approved: start within 3 working days, first version within 7. Two revision rounds are included on the deliverable. Feedback is due within 5 working days.

## Rush

Rush is not a tone of voice in the request. Rush applies only with written confirmation from the account lead. The client asking for it in Harbor is not that confirmation. The account lead writes it.

When it is confirmed: start within 1 working day, first version within 3 working days. The rush rate is the rate in force on the date of written confirmation. This page does not restate the percentage. Look up the rate that applies on that date, and use that one even if the work is delivered after the rate changes. Partner clients keep their place in the schedule. A rush on another account does not jump ahead of them.
