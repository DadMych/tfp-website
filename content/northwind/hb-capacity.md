# Handbook: capacity planning

*Internal. Last updated 4 March 2026. This is how the week gets booked. It is not a utilisation target and it is not something we show clients.*

## The number we plan with

Designers are planned at 26 billable hours per week. That is the load we put on the calendar. It is not a promise that every week lands on exactly that, and it is not a score. Account leads are not planned on this number.

When you estimate a request, add a 15% buffer before you tell the client the hours or put the work on a week. Share the estimate in Harbor and wait for the client to approve it. A number you only said on a call is not the estimate.

If a week is already at 26 billable hours, the next request moves. Don't tuck it into the evenings and call it fine.

## Who gets the week first

Partner clients get priority in scheduling. When a Partner request and another request want the same days, the Partner work is placed first and the other start moves, inside the turnaround we actually promised. Say that out loud to the other client. Don't hope they won't notice.

Kestrel is on Partner. Treat their booked work that way. Don't invent a second queue in your head.

## Ordinary requests

On a retainer the standard turnaround is: start within 3 working days, first version within 7. Plan from those, not from "soon".

Kickoff for a new agreement still has to fall within 10 working days of signing. Hold that time when the agreement is signed, not when the file is convenient.

## Rush

A rush starts within 1 working day and the first version is within 3. It has to be confirmed in writing by the account lead. Before you write that confirmation, check capacity with Oleksij. If he says the week can't take it, the answer to the client is no, or a later start. Not a quiet yes.

The rate is the one in force on the date of the written confirmation. From 1 March 2026 that is 40%. A rush confirmed before 1 March stays at 25% even if we deliver it in March.

On a retainer, rush is charged in hours. A 10-hour rush uses 14 hours. Plan 14, not 10, or you will blow the week and call it a mystery.

## When the plan breaks

If a start is going to be late, tell the account lead the same day. Moving a Partner request to make room for a non-Partner rush is not a favour you can do on your own. Priority runs the other way, and the rush still needs Oleksij.
