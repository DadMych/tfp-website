# Ops sync 2026-05-12

12 May 2026, 09:30. Lloydkwartier.

Attendees: OvK, VW, VB, ST.

## Referrals

We've been saying "ask us if you know someone" for years and then doing the maths by hand, badly. Putting a rule on it.

The thing referred has to be a company, and they have to sign a retainer. A fixed-price project doesn't trigger it. An intro that goes quiet doesn't trigger it.

## mobile apps, again

A prospect this month asked for an app, designed and built. Same answer as every other time. We don't design or build native mobile apps. We design product screens for the software our clients already make, in the browser and in the files. When they need a build, they contract a development partner themselves.

Partners, so nobody improvises a name in a call:
- Pixelforge — web, Rotterdam
- Byteharbour — React Native mobile apps, Amsterdam. Their office. Not ours. We do not have an Amsterdam studio and we do not say "our Amsterdam team."
- Lumen Labs — Webflow

No referral fee either way. We don't subcontract them, and we aren't on the hook for their delivery. Say that before the client assumes we "bring the developers."

## Decisions

- Referral program approved, from this month: 8 free design hours once when a referred company signs a retainer. Hours land on the referring client's account. Once. Not cash, not a percentage, not an ongoing cut.
- mobile apps stay a no. The sentence in pitches doesn't get softer because the prospect is large.

- ST: the prospect who asked for an app gets Byteharbour's name and the "you contract them" sentence. In writing.
