# Meetings and time zones

We are in Rotterdam. A lot of our clients are not. Meetings stay inside the studio day, and we would rather record a walkthrough than drag you into a call that only needs your eyes.

## When we can meet

The studio works 09:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday. That is the window for meetings. We do not book earlier or later to chase a timezone, and we do not book on days the studio is closed: Dutch public holidays, and 24 December through 2 January.

If you write on a closed day, the request waits until the next working day. A meeting does the same.

## North America

For clients in North America we offer slots at 16:00–18:00 CET. That is the overlap we can actually staff. 16:00 CET is the start of it. We will not put you in a 09:00 CET meeting because a calendar happened to have a gap there.

Say which hours on your side are realistic when we schedule the kickoff. If 16:00 CET is still too early for the west coast, we will not pretend a live meeting is the only way to move the work.

## Loom, when a call is the wrong tool

When the point is to watch us walk through a file, we record it on Loom instead of holding a meeting. You can watch it when your morning actually starts, and the people who could not attend still see the same thing.

We keep live meetings for decisions: a kickoff, a review where you have to choose, a handoff where developers need to ask something. A presentation of screens is a Loom.

## How long the set calls are

The kickoff is 60 minutes. We use it to confirm scope, who approves, and how you will send feedback. Come with the people who can answer those things. We will not reconvene the kickoff because a decision-maker watches the recording later and disagrees.

A handoff, later, is a different call: 45 minutes, one per deliverable. Developer questions after that handoff are free for 14 days, through Harbor. It is not a second kickoff, and it is not 60 minutes again by default.
