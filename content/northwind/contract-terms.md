# Contract terms

This is a plain-English summary of our standard agreement. If anything here differs from your signed agreement, the signed agreement wins.

## Invoices and payment

Retainers are invoiced on the first working day of each month. Fixed-price projects are invoiced 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery. Payment terms are 14 days from the invoice date.

Invoices more than 30 days overdue pause all work on the account until they are paid. We charge statutory interest on late payments; we don't charge separate late fees.

## Unused hours

Unused retainer hours roll over to the next month only, then expire. Rolled-over hours are used first. Unused hours are never paid out or refunded, including when you cancel.

## Cancelling

You can cancel a retainer with 30 days' written notice, by email to your account lead. Annual retainers can be cancelled the same way; the unused months are refunded minus the annual discount you received for them.

Fixed-price projects can be cancelled at any time. The kickoff invoice is not refunded; work done after it is not charged.

## Who owns the work

You own the final delivered files once the last invoice for them is paid. Until then we hold the rights. Drafts and rejected concepts stay ours and are never reused for another client's brand.

We may show finished work in our portfolio after launch, unless your agreement has a confidentiality clause. You can ask for one at signing at no extra cost.
