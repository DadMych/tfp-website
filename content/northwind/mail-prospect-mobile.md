Thank you for writing. Northwind is a design studio for software companies. We design product screens for web apps, and we do brand, web and design systems. We do not build native mobile apps, and we do not design them either. An iOS app, including the screens, is outside what we take on.

For the native app, the studio we point people to is Byteharbour. They build React Native mobile apps. Their office is in Amsterdam. That office is theirs, not a Northwind office. You would contract them directly. We do not subcontract them, and we do not take a referral fee.

If you also need design for a web product, we can talk about that on its own. It would be a separate question from the iOS app. An intro call is the usual next step for web work. I will not fold the app into a Northwind proposal.

Best,
