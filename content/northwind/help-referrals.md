# Referring another company

From May 2026, clients can refer another company to Northwind. The benefit is design time on your own account. It is not a finder's fee.

## What you receive

When a company you refer signs a retainer, you get 8 free design hours, added once to your account. Once means once. A second retainer, a renewal, or a larger plan later does not add the hours again.

The trigger is a retainer. Starter, Studio or Partner all count, because each of them is a retainer. A fixed-price project on its own does not. If the company you introduce only buys a Brand Sprint, a Website Sprint or a Design System Audit, the hours are not added.

## What you do not receive

There is no cash. We do not pay a bounty, and we do not split their invoice with you. There is no ongoing commission in later months. The 8 free design hours are the whole of the program.

We also do not discount their plan because you introduced them. Their price is their agreement. Your hours are yours. The two do not mix, and this is not the non-profit discount or the annual prepayment discount under another name.

## How to do it

Tell your account lead the company's name, and that you are the person referring them, before they sign. We do not issue codes, and we do not run a form you have to remember after the fact.

The hours are added once the referred company has signed a retainer. They are design hours, the same kind of hours your plan is made of. If you are not sure they were added, ask your account lead to look at the account. Do not invoice us for them, and do not expect a payment.
