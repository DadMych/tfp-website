# Using Harbor, the client portal

Harbor is where a request, a file and an invoice belong. It launched in May 2025 and replaced email-only requests. If it is not in Harbor, we do not treat it as submitted.

## Opening a request

Use one request form per deliverable. A homepage and a signup flow are two forms, even if you thought of them on the same morning. Put the brief in the form: what you need, what "done" looks like, and anything we should not change.

Attach the files to that request. A deck sitting in an old thread is not attached, and we will not go hunting for it. Reference material, a login note, a spreadsheet of labels: if we need it, it goes on the form.

Your plan caps how many requests can be active at once. Starter allows 1, Studio allows 2, Partner allows 4. If you are already at that number, ask your account lead before you open another.

## What the status means

A request moves through four statuses:

- **Queued.** We have it. It is not being designed yet.
- **In progress.** Someone is in the file.
- **In review.** It is with you. In February 2026 we renamed "Waiting for client" to In review. Same moment in the work, current name.
- **Done.** The request is closed.

Watch the status on the request rather than asking in a side channel whether it has started. Standard retainer work starts within 3 working days, with a first version within 7 working days. The status is how you see that happening.

## Approving, or sending it back

When the status is In review, open the request and look at what we posted. Approve it there, or send the feedback for that round on that same request. Approval is what lets us call the deliverable finished. Comments left only in a meeting do not replace the request.

Two rounds of revisions are included per deliverable. How to bundle a round is covered in the feedback article. Harbor is the place the round is recorded.

## Invoices

Invoices are on the same account. Open Harbor and go to invoices when finance asks for a PDF. Questions about the bill itself go to Vladislava Wolinska. Questions about the work go to your account lead.
