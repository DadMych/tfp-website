## Fees in hours

| Situation on a retainer | Hours charged |
|---|---|
| Ordinary hour | 1 hour of the monthly budget |
| Rush (40% fee) | Multiplier 1.4. A 10-hour rush uses 14 hours |

The rush fee is 40% from 1 March 2026, so on retainers the multiplier is 1.4: a 10-hour rush uses 14 hours. A rush confirmed in writing before that date keeps 25%, including a confirmation dated 25 February 2026 that is delivered in March. Rush still needs that written confirmation from the account lead, and it means a start within 1 working day and a first version within 3 working days.
