# Proposal: Halden Health design system audit

For Halden Health. Dated 30 January 2026. Prices exclude VAT.

## Summary

A Design System Audit, code NW-AUD-DS, €3,800, two weeks. Michiel Dembitski reviews the Figma library and writes up what he finds, with a prioritised list. The fee is for that written review, not including the fixes. Halden's own team, or a later retainer with us, does the fixes. This proposal does not do them.

## Scope

In scope: reading the library, a written review, and a prioritised list of what to fix first. Tokens, components, and variants are in that review where they exist in the file. The accessibility target we design to is WCAG 2.2 AA, and the review will use that as the bar. The review is not a certification, and we do not write production code.

Out of scope, and called out here so it is not assumed later: redrawing components, building missing ones, and applying the list. The €3,800 is the written review, not including that work. If the list should be carried out by us, that is a separate Studio retainer, described below, and only if you want it.

Two revision rounds are included on the written review. Feedback within 5 working days. After 10 working days of silence the round is accepted.

## Team

Michiel Dembitski, design systems lead, does the review. Kickoff within 10 working days of signing.

## Timeline (week by week)

- Week 1. Kickoff, and Michiel reads the library in the Figma file you share.
- Week 2. The written review, the prioritised list, and a 45-minute handoff call to walk the list through.

Two revision rounds are included on that review, and feedback is due within 5 working days. Developer questions on the handoff are free for 14 days. Doing the fixes is still not part of either week.

## Investment

| Item | Code | Price (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Design System Audit, 2 weeks, written review only | NW-AUD-DS | €3,800 |
| Optional, not part of this fee: Studio retainer if you want us to make the fixes afterwards | NW-RET-M | €5,800 per month, 64 hours, 2 active requests |

The audit is invoiced 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery. Payment terms are 14 days. The Studio retainer, if you take it, is a different agreement, invoiced on the first working day of each month. It is not started by paying the audit.

## Terms

If you stop the audit, the kickoff invoice is not refunded. You own the written review once it is paid for. This proposal includes a confidentiality clause at no charge: we will not show the library, or the review, in the portfolio. Files are kept 24 months after the last invoice unless you ask for deletion sooner, in which case we confirm deletion within 10 working days. Invoices are kept 7 years.
