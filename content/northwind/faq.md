# Frequently asked questions

## Can we pause a retainer?

Yes, once per year for up to two months. Tell your account lead at least 14 days before the next invoice date. Hours don't accrue during a pause, and rolled-over hours expire at the start of it.

## Can we switch plans?

Yes. Upgrades apply from the next invoice. Downgrades apply after 30 days' notice, like a cancellation.

## Do you work with startups?

Yes. About half of our clients are seed to Series B software companies. We don't offer equity-for-work deals.

## Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Registered non-profits get 15% off retainers. It cannot be combined with the annual discount.

## Can we hire one of your designers?

Our agreement includes a non-solicitation clause for 12 months after the project ends. If you want to hire someone, talk to the studio director first; a placement fee applies.

## Do you do naming?

Yes, as an add-on to a Brand Sprint for €2,500, covering up to three name candidates with basic trademark search guidance. We are not lawyers and the final trademark check is yours.
