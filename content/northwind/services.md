# What Northwind does

Northwind Studio is a design agency of 16 people, based in Rotterdam and working with clients across Europe and North America. We design brands, websites and design systems for software companies.

## Services

- **Brand identity.** Logo, type, colour, tone of voice and a brand book. Usually sold as a Brand Sprint.
- **Web design.** Marketing sites and product landing pages, designed in Figma and handed off to your developers.
- **Design systems.** Component libraries in Figma with design tokens that your engineers can import.
- **Product design.** Screens and flows for web apps, on a monthly retainer.

## What we don't do

We do not write production code. We do not build mobile apps, run ad campaigns, or manage social media accounts. If you need developers, ask your account lead for our partner list; we hand off to partner studios but we don't subcontract them and we are not responsible for their work.

We don't do print production beyond print-ready files. We don't do video or motion beyond simple logo animations included in a Brand Sprint.

## Who you work with

Every client gets one account lead, who is your single point of contact, and one senior designer who owns the work. Larger retainers add a second designer. You can see who is on your project in the client portal under "Team".
