# Revisions policy (2024, superseded)

Superseded. This policy is no longer in force. It applied before 1 January 2025 and must not be quoted on a current project, a renewal, or a proposal. The date on this page is 1 March 2024. The rule itself ended on 1 January 2025.

## What a client got

Each deliverable included three rounds of revisions. A round was one pass of feedback and the updated files that followed it. The client did not have to buy those three rounds; they were part of the deliverable.

That is the whole of the included allowance in this old policy: three rounds, then a fee.

## The extra round

A further round, past those three rounds, was €400. The fee was for the extra round on a fixed-price deliverable. This page does not describe retainers, because the retainer hour rules are not what this old page was for.

€400 is the figure in this document only. It is not a figure to type into a 2025 or 2026 quote. Anyone who finds €400 in an old email and wants to match it is using a superseded fee.

## What "superseded" means here

Superseded on 1 January 2025. From that date the studio stopped including three rounds. Staff who still have this page bookmarked should close it. If a client signed under the old allowance, their signed agreement says what they get; this archived page does not reopen that allowance for anyone else.

This document is archived. It is no longer in force. It does not set the feedback window, the rush fee, or any price in the current rate card.
