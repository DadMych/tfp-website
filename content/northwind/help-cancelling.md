# Ending a retainer

You can end a retainer. The notice has to be written, the hours you have not used do not come back as money, and the Figma seats close on their own clock. Read this before you send the email, so the email says the right thing.

## How to give notice

Email your account lead. That email is the notice. A remark in a meeting, or a line in a Harbor request about a single deliverable, is not.

The notice is 30 days' written notice. The retainer runs through that notice. We do not stop on the afternoon the email arrives, and we do not invent a shorter exit because the month is quiet.

Say the date you want the retainer to end, and which plan it is. If you are unsure of the date, ask your account lead to reply with the end date they will use. Their written reply is the confirmation. Keep it.

## Hours are not refunded

Unused hours are not refunded. That includes hours left in the current month and hours that rolled over from the previous one. Rolled-over hours last one month and then expire. They are never paid out, including when you cancel.

Money is a different line from hours, and only if you prepaid the year. On an annual retainer, unused months are refunded minus the discount you received for those months. The hours inside a month you did pay for are still not refunded. Months and hours are not the same balance.

This page is about retainers. On a fixed-price project, the kickoff invoice is not refunded either. Work after that invoice, if you stop, is not charged.

## A pause is not a cancellation

If you want a gap rather than an ending, you can pause once per year, for up to two months. Give 14 days' notice before the next invoice. Pausing is not the same email as cancelling. Say which one you mean.

## Files, and the Figma seats

Export the Figma files before access is removed. We remove Figma access 30 days after the end of the engagement. Those 30 days are for export. They are not an extra month of design, and they are not the same 30 days as your notice.

You own the final files once they are paid for. Download them, and download anything else you need from Harbor, inside that window.

Separately, we keep files for 24 months after the last invoice. You can ask us to delete them; we confirm deletion within 10 working days. Invoices themselves are kept for 7 years. Deleting the work does not delete the invoices.
