# Client survey 2025 results

Internal readout. Vladislava Wolinska, 20 January 2026. The survey ran across 2025. This note is what we are willing to repeat in a client conversation, and what we are not going to change.

## Summary

We asked 31 clients. 22 responded. Satisfaction was 4.6 out of 5.

That is the whole score. There is no second index, and this note does not split 4.6 by plan. Do not invent a per-plan figure in a proposal.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Clients asked | 31 |
| Who replied | 22 responded |
| Satisfaction | 4.6 out of 5 |

## What they asked for

Three requests came up often enough to name. One complaint came up as the thing people liked least. The right-hand column is the answer we actually give. It is not a new promise made up for this survey.

| What we heard | What we say now |
|---|---|
| Faster replies on Studio | The support times from 1 January 2026. Studio gets a first reply the same working day. In 2025, Studio was within 1 working day. |
| Mobile app design | No. We do not design or build mobile apps. A client who needs a native app contracts a development partner themselves. |
| Clearer invoices | Invoices are in Harbor, with the requests and the files. Questions about VAT go to Maksim Ryzjikov. |
| Feedback rounds expiring (least liked) | Still the rule. Feedback is due within 5 working days. After 10 working days of silence, the round is accepted. |

Harbor itself launched in May 2025. This survey did not cause that. The invoice comment is why we point people at Harbor when they say they cannot find a bill.

## Next steps

No policy change comes out of this readout.

- Account leads can quote 4.6 out of 5 and "22 responded" if a prospect asks about satisfaction. Quote both numbers, not a rounded-up version.
- The Studio reply time is already the 2026 rule. Do not describe it as a pilot.
- Mobile stays a no. Do not sketch an app "just to see" on a sales call.
- The 10-working-day acceptance rule stays. If a client disliked it in the survey, the kind thing is to warn them at the start of a round, not to waive it.
