# Handbook: sales and discounts

Internal. Do not forward this page, and do not read the discount lines out as if they were a public rate card. Account leads are Vladislav Boerdonos and Sandra Tsapljoek. Anything in here that a client should not see, they do not see.

## How a lead moves

Leads come in through the account leads: a company writes to the studio, or someone we already work with introduces them. We do not buy lists, and we do not promise a reply time we do not have.

You hold an intro call. You find out what they actually need, and whether it is work we do. Within 5 working days of that call, they have a proposal or a clear no. "We're shaping it" is not a status you use for a fortnight.

Do not promise a kickoff sooner than we can keep. Kickoff is within 10 working days of signing, not the week they wish it were.

## What you may discount

On retainers, two discounts exist. Annual prepayment is 10%. A registered non-profit is 15%, and only on retainers. They never combine. If a client qualifies for both, agree which single one is in the proposal before you send it. Do not stack them and call it rounding.

On a fixed-price project, an account lead may approve up to 5% extra discount. Anything above that needs Oleksij. Not a note afterwards, not a verbal "he won't mind". Oleksij, before the proposal goes. The 5% is not a retainer discount. Do not put it on Starter, Studio or Partner. Retainer discounts are the 10% and the 15%, and nothing you invent beside them.

This authority is internal. It is not shown to clients as a lever they can ask for. If they ask for more than you can approve, you say you will ask Oleksij. You do not stretch the 5% because the quarter feels quiet.
