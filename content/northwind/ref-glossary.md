# Glossary

Terms as we use them at Northwind Studio. This page is dated 10 November 2025. A later page wins where a rule has changed since.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| active request | An active request is one piece of client work open at the same time. The plan caps how many may be open. From 1 January 2026 the caps are 1 on Starter, 2 on Studio, and 4 on Partner. |
| round | One pass of revisions on a deliverable: consolidated feedback, then our update. Two rounds are included per deliverable. Before 1 January 2025 the included number was three. Feedback is due within 5 working days. After 10 working days of silence the round is accepted. An extra round on a fixed-price project is €450. |
| rollover | rollover is what happens to unused retainer hours. They move to the next month only, are used first, and then expire. They are never refunded. |
| rush | A faster request. We start within 1 working day and deliver a first version within 3 working days. It applies only after the account lead confirms it in writing. On the date of this page the rush fee is 25%. |
| deliverable | A distinct agreed output, such as a logo, a set of page templates, or a written audit. Each deliverable carries its own rounds and its own handoff. |
| handoff | The moment we walk developers through a deliverable. One handoff call per deliverable, 45 minutes. Developer questions are free for 14 days after handoff. A further call is €150 per hour outside a retainer, or it uses retainer hours. |
| design QA | A check of built pages against the designs. Included in the Website Sprint. Otherwise it uses retainer hours, or €150 per hour outside a retainer. |
| design tokens | Colour, type, and space decisions, delivered as JSON in the W3C design tokens format. |
| account lead | The person at Northwind who owns the relationship. The account leads are Vladislav Boerdonos and Sandra Tsapljoek. Written notice, including a rush confirmation, goes to that person. |
| Harbor | The client portal for requests, files, and invoices. Launched May 2025. It replaced email-only requests. Status names on this date: Queued, In progress, Waiting for client, Done. |
| crit | The weekly design critique, Wednesdays at 14:00. Internal. Designers bring work that is not finished. |
| Partner plan | A retainer that starts on 1 January 2026. It was set at the pricing meeting of 4 November 2025 and is not on sale on the date of this page. From 1 January 2026: code NW-RET-L, €11,200 a month, 130 hours, 4 active requests, a second senior designer, and a monthly review call with the studio director. |
| approver | The one person the client names to send the feedback for a round. One list, from that person, rather than notes from several people treated as several rounds. |

Hours on a retainer are a budget. The studio is in Lloydkwartier, Rotterdam, and is closed from 24 December through 2 January and on Dutch public holidays.
