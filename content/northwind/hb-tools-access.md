# Handbook: tools and access

*Internal. Last updated 5 August 2025. Ops owns accounts. If you need access, ask ops. Don't share your login because it's faster.*

## What you get

On your first day you get a Google Workspace account. That is email, calendar, and the drive. The rest of the studio hangs off it.

You also get, as the work requires:

- Figma, for design files
- Notion, for the internal wiki and client pages
- Linear, for tasks
- Loom, for walkthroughs
- Harbor, for client requests, files, and invoices

You don't get access to a client project "just in case". The account lead or the designer running the file asks ops when you actually need it.

## 2FA and passwords

2FA is required on Figma and on Google before you open client work. Set it up on day one. SMS is not the thing to rely on if you have an authenticator app. If you lose the second factor, tell ops the same day. Don't ask a teammate to stay logged in for you.

Shared logins, the few we have, live in the password manager. They don't live in Slack, in a Notion comment, or in a document called "passwords". If someone pastes one into chat, delete it and put it back in the password manager. Personal passwords stay in your own manager. Don't reuse the studio ones.

## While you're here

Lock the screen when you leave the desk. Office days are busy and the room is not private. Client files stay in the tools above. Don't drop a Figma export on a personal account to "finish it tonight" and don't put client material in a tool we haven't approved.

If a client needs their own Figma access, that request goes through Harbor. This page is about staff accounts, not theirs.

## When someone leaves

Offboarding removes access the same day. Not Friday, not "once the files are copied", not after we've had a drink. Ops removes Google Workspace, Figma, Notion, Linear, Loom, and Harbor on the day the person leaves.

If you are the one leaving, don't do a farewell export of client files onto a laptop. Hand the links to your account lead.

## Don't mix this up with client access

Staff access ends the same day. Client Figma access is different: we remove it 30 days after the engagement ends. Those are two rules. Applying the client rule to a leaver, or the staff rule to a client, is how we either lock a client out early or leave a leaver in the file.
