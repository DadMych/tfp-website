# Northwind Studio — canon

Every generated document must agree with this file. Northwind Studio is fictional.

## Company
- Design agency, founded 2017 by Oleksij van Koeropatkin (studio director). One office: Lloydkwartier, Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Many clients are remote (Europe, North America).
- 16 people. Works for software companies: brand identity, web design, design systems, product design. Does NOT write production code, build mobile apps, run ads, manage social media, or do video beyond simple logo animations.
- Working hours 09:00–18:00 CET, Monday–Friday. Closed 24 December–2 January and on Dutch public holidays.
- Tools: Figma (design), the client portal called "Harbor" (requests, files, invoices; launched May 2025, replaced email-only requests), Notion (internal wiki), Linear (internal task tracking), Loom (walkthrough videos), Google Workspace.

## Policies
- Payment terms 14 days. Retainers invoiced on the first working day of the month; fixed-price 50% at kickoff, 50% on delivery. More than 30 days overdue pauses work. Statutory interest, no separate late fees.
- Unused retainer hours roll over one month only, then expire; never refunded. (One-time exception: Kestrel Analytics got a two-month rollover in March 2026 because of a delay on Northwind's side. Not policy.)
- Cancel retainer: 30 days' written notice to the account lead. Annual: unused months refunded minus the discount received for them. Fixed-price: kickoff invoice not refunded.
- Pause: once per year, up to two months, 14 days' notice before the next invoice.
- Revisions: two rounds included per deliverable, extra round €450 on fixed-price projects; both since 1 January 2025. Before that the policy was three rounds and an extra round cost €400. Feedback within 5 working days; after 10 working days of silence the round is accepted.
- Rush: start within 1 working day, first version within 3; must be confirmed in writing by the account lead. Rush fee 40% from 1 March 2026 (decided at the ops meeting on 17 February 2026); 25% before. On retainers charged in hours (10-hour rush uses 14 hours).
- Standard turnaround on retainers: start within 3 working days, first version within 7.
- Support first reply: Starter within 2 working days, Studio same working day, Partner within 4 working hours. Urgent line Partner only (working hours plus 07:00–09:00 CET). Before 2026 (old SLA) Studio got replies within 1 working day.
- Files kept 24 months after last invoice; deletion on request confirmed within 10 working days; invoices kept 7 years. Figma access removed 30 days after the end. Data hosted in the EU.
- IP: client owns final files once paid. Portfolio use allowed unless a confidentiality clause (free at signing).
- Non-solicitation 12 months; placement fee applies (amount not public).
- Handoff: one 45-minute handoff call per deliverable; developer questions free for 14 days after handoff. Tokens delivered as JSON in the W3C design tokens format.
- Accessibility target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- VAT: Dutch clients 21%; EU business clients outside the Netherlands reverse charge.
- Referral program (from May 2026): 8 free design hours once when a referred company signs a retainer.
- Harbor request statuses: Queued, In progress, In review (renamed from "Waiting for client" in February 2026), Done.
- Weekly design crit Wednesdays 14:00.
- Rush rate: the rate in force on the date of the written confirmation applies (a rush confirmed 25 February 2026 is 25% even if delivered in March).
- Downgrade: rolled-over hours are capped to the new plan's monthly hours. Upgrade: rolled-over hours carry over.
- Closures 2026: 1–2 January, Good Friday 3 April, Easter Monday 6 April, King's Day 27 April, Liberation Day 5 May, Ascension Day 14 May, Whit Monday 25 May, 24–31 December.
